The best attribution software for real estate in 2027 is the one that connects each ad to a visit, an appointment and a closed sale, not just to a lead. For brokerages in the US, Follow Up Boss and Lofty report lead sources inside the CRM; CallRail and Ruler Analytics add call and revenue tracking; HubSpot and Kommo work as the CRM layer; and Admira fits developers and brokerages that sell through WhatsApp and need ad-level revenue attribution across Meta, Google and TikTok. Prices were checked in November 2026 on each vendor's page, and the list is updated for 2027 planning.

This guide is published by Admira, a marketing attribution platform. We include Admira where it fits and state its limits. Real estate sales take weeks or months and usually close offline, so the right tool depends on where your team logs the sale.

How we chose these tools

We looked at tools that real estate developers, brokerages and agent teams use to answer one question: which campaigns produced sales. We checked each vendor's pricing page in November 2026 and read product pages for lead source, call and revenue reporting. We did not use review-site rankings. Where a vendor hides pricing, we say "not published". We left out listing portals and lead-generation services because they sell leads rather than measure them.

Admira: best for ad-to-sale attribution with WhatsApp

Admira ties leads from Meta, Google, TikTok, Click-to-WhatsApp and the website to visits, appointments and closed sales in the CRM. It compares first-touch, last-touch, linear and U-shaped models across long buying cycles and shows CAC, ROAS, ROI and pipeline by campaign and ad. It connects to Kommo, HubSpot, Salesforce, Clientify and ActiveCampaign. KW, a real estate customer, reports 25% faster closing cycles (Admira customers). Plans start at $199/month, Growth is $399 and Custom $1,500 (pricing).

Limits: call tracking numbers are not among its listed features, it is a young brand with few public reviews, and it is not an MMM consultancy. Details on the real estate attribution page.

Follow Up Boss: best CRM source reporting for US teams

Follow Up Boss Grow costs $69 per user per month ($58 on yearly billing). Pro is $499/month for 10 users with unlimited calling and texting, and Platform is $1,000/month for 30 users. Every plan includes agent and lead source performance reporting (Follow Up Boss pricing).

Pros: widely used by agent teams, strong lead routing. Limits: the pricing page does not describe ROI or ad-level attribution; source is usually the portal or site name, not the campaign.

Lofty: best all-in-one brokerage platform

Lofty offers Agent, Team, Broker and Enterprise plans, all on "request pricing". The comparison table shows reporting on every plan, plus AI lead scoring and optional lead-gen programs, but it does not describe source or ROI reports (Lofty pricing).

Pros: CRM, website and lead gen in one. Limits: no public prices; confirm attribution reports in a demo.

CallRail: best for call-heavy agents

CallRail's Lead Tracking plan starts at $50/month with call tracking, recording and transcription, including 5 local numbers and 250 minutes. Lead Tracking Complete at $95 adds form tracking and multi-touch cost-per-lead reporting (CallRail pricing).

Pros: tracking numbers per campaign or portal. Limits: listed for the US, Canada, the UK and Australia; you still need the CRM to know which calls closed.

Ruler Analytics: best for revenue attribution in the UK and EU

Ruler's Small plan is £299 ($400) a month for up to 10,000 visits, with multi-touch attribution, CRM opportunity and revenue stage reporting, and dynamic call tracking listed among its features (Ruler pricing).

Pros: ties web, call and form leads to CRM revenue. Limits: priced above call-only tools.

HubSpot: best for developers with a marketing team

HubSpot attribution reports need Marketing Hub Professional or Enterprise. Professional starts at $800/month on annual billing plus a $3,000 onboarding fee. It works when sales reps keep deal stages current; offline sales still need to be logged by hand or synced.

Kommo: best chat-first CRM in LATAM

Kommo costs $25 per user per month on Base, $35 on Advanced and $45 on Pro, with a 6-month minimum. Pro lists sales and marketing analytics and campaign ROI tracking (Kommo pricing). Kommo can save Meta ad IDs on leads that message from ads. Our guide on measuring sales from ads in Kommo shows how.

Limits: source capture depends on UTMs and ad permissions being set up correctly.

How do you set up attribution for real estate?

  1. Define your funnel stages: lead, contacted, visit or showroom appointment, offer or reservation, signed, closed.
  2. Capture source on every lead: UTMs in forms, ad IDs on WhatsApp chats, tracking numbers on calls.
  3. Make agents update stages in the CRM the same day, with the sale value on closing.
  4. Choose an attribution model that fits long cycles (U-shaped or linear rather than last click).
  5. Send visits and closings back to Meta and Google as conversions so campaigns optimize for buyers.
  6. Review cost per visit and cost per sale by campaign monthly. See how to measure cost per sale in real estate.

What about Meta's housing ad rules?

For housing ads in the US and some other countries, Meta requires the Housing special ad category, which restricts targeting options such as age, gender, ZIP codes and lookalike audiences (Meta Advertising Standards). This limits how precisely you can target, which makes measuring real sales by campaign more important. Check whether the category applies in your country before launching. Who this list is for, and who it is not for

For: developers, brokerages and agent teams that spend on paid ads and log sales in a CRM. Not for: single agents working only referrals, or teams that do not record visits and closings. Without those stages, attribution stops at the lead.

FAQ

What is the best CRM for real estate lead source tracking?

In the US, Follow Up Boss includes lead source reporting on every plan from $69 per user per month. In LATAM, chat-first teams often use Kommo from $25 per user per month (checked November 2026).

Can I attribute a home sale that closes months after the ad?

Yes, if the lead keeps its original source in the CRM and the sale is logged on the same record. Use a model that credits the first touch as well as the last.

Should real estate teams optimize Meta ads for leads or sales?

For sales or visits where possible. Lead-optimized campaigns often bring cheap, low-intent leads. Sending visits and closings back to Meta helps it find buyers.

Does Click-to-WhatsApp work for real estate?

It often brings fewer but more engaged conversations. Track it from chat to sale to judge it. See our Click-to-WhatsApp ads guide.

Is Follow Up Boss an attribution tool?

It is a CRM with lead source reporting. It does not describe ad-level or multi-touch attribution on its pricing page.
