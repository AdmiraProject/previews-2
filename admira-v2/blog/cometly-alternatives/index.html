The best Cometly alternatives in 2026 are Admira for lead-gen and WhatsApp-heavy sales teams, Hyros for info and call-funnel businesses, Ruler Analytics and SegMetrics for budget-conscious lead-gen and funnel tracking, Wicked Reports and Triple Whale for ecommerce, and Dreamdata or HockeyStack for B2B SaaS. The right pick depends on where your sale closes: on a checkout page, in a CRM, on a call, or in a WhatsApp chat.

Cometly itself is a capable tool. Its pricing page lists a pixel, server-side tracking, multi-touch attribution, a Conversion API, customizable dashboards, an AI Ads Manager and 70+ integrations on its Core plan. But it no longer publishes prices: both plans are quoted based on monthly pageviews (Cometly pricing, checked November 2026). Teams usually look elsewhere for pricing clarity, CRM depth, Spanish support or a better fit for their sales motion.

Why do teams look for Cometly alternatives?

  • No published price. You need a sales call to know what you'll pay, and the bill scales with pageviews.
  • Sales happen outside the website. If deals close in a CRM, on the phone or in WhatsApp, the quality of the CRM integration matters more than the pixel.
  • Regional needs. Teams in Mexico and LATAM often need Click-to-WhatsApp attribution, CRMs like Kommo or Clientify, and support in Spanish.
  • Different category. Shopify brands may want profit and LTV analytics; B2B SaaS teams may want account-level journeys.

Which Cometly alternative fits each use case?

Admira: best for lead-gen and WhatsApp sales

Admira connects Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and Click-to-WhatsApp leads to revenue in HubSpot, Salesforce, Kommo, Clientify or ActiveCampaign. It includes attribution models, buyer journeys, unlimited dashboards and a Conversions API that sends CRM events back to ad platforms server-side. Pricing is public: Starter $199/month (10,000 unique visitors, 1 seat), Growth $399/month (30,000 visitors, 6 seats), Custom $1,500/month. Tracking is cookieless by default. Limits: it's a young brand with few public reviews, and pure Shopify stores may prefer an ecommerce-first tool. See our full Admira vs Cometly comparison.

Hyros: best for info products and call funnels

Hyros focuses on ad tracking for SaaS, ecommerce, info and education businesses, plus B2B calls, and sends sales data back to Meta and Google. Its pricing page shows one "Custom" plan quoted on a roughly 15-minute call and sized to the revenue it tracks (Hyros pricing). Limits: no published price, and costs rise as tracked revenue grows. Compare in Hyros vs Cometly.

Ruler Analytics: best for UK and EU lead-gen teams

Ruler publishes indicative pricing from $400/month for up to 10k monthly visits, rising to $2,000/month on Advanced, which adds marketing mix modelling and an AI agent. Every plan lists multi-touch attribution, a dedicated CS manager and white-glove onboarding (Ruler pricing). Limits: priced in GBP first, and the jump between tiers is steep as traffic grows.

SegMetrics: best budget option for funnels

SegMetrics starts at $57/month (up to 10K contacts) with Grow at $197 and Scale at $397, which adds server-side tracking (SegMetrics pricing). Limits: built around email and funnel tools, less around sales-team CRMs or WhatsApp.

Wicked Reports and Triple Whale: best for ecommerce

Wicked Reports' Measure plan is $499/month for brands with up to $2.5M in annual revenue (Wicked Reports pricing). Triple Whale offers a free plan and GMV-based paid plans. Both fit online-checkout businesses better than lead-gen. See Triple Whale alternatives.

Dreamdata and HockeyStack: best for B2B SaaS

Dreamdata has a free plan and a custom Advanced plan with AI and custom attribution models (Dreamdata pricing). HockeyStack doesn't publish pricing. Both suit SaaS companies tracking accounts over long cycles more than paid-social lead-gen.

How should you choose a Cometly alternative?

  1. Locate your sale. Write down where revenue is recorded: Shopify, a CRM, a phone system or WhatsApp. Eliminate tools that can't read that source.
  2. List your channels. Make sure Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and any WhatsApp or LinkedIn campaigns are supported.
  3. Check the CRM integration in a demo. Ask to see a real deal from your CRM matched to its ad touchpoints.
  4. Confirm the Conversions API. Sending qualified leads and sales back to Meta and Google is often where the ROI comes from.
  5. Get the price in writing on your numbers. Give every vendor the same traffic, revenue and seat count.
  6. Run a two-week trial or pilot. Compare one month of closed deals against what each tool attributes.

The cheapest line isn't the point. The point is that the tool must read revenue where it actually lives. A tool that costs $200 less but can't see WhatsApp sales will report the wrong winners.

When should you stay with Cometly?

Switching isn't always right. Stay if Cometly already reads your CRM revenue correctly, your team trusts the reports, and the quoted price works for your traffic. Its Enterprise plan also lists CRM and warehouse sync, Snowflake and BigQuery support and custom integration builds, which matters for larger data teams. Switching tools mid-quarter also resets your comparison baseline, so plan the move between reporting periods.

FAQ

What is the best alternative to Cometly?

It depends on your sales motion. For lead-gen and WhatsApp sales in LATAM, Admira is built for that case; for info and call funnels, Hyros; for Shopify brands, Triple Whale or Wicked Reports; for B2B SaaS, Dreamdata or HockeyStack.

How much does Cometly cost?

Cometly doesn't publish prices. As of November 2026, its Core and Enterprise plans are quoted based on monthly pageviews, with a 20% discount for annual billing.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Cometly?

SegMetrics starts at $57/month and Admira at $199/month, both with public pricing. Whether that's cheaper depends on your Cometly quote, which scales with pageviews.

Which Cometly alternative works with WhatsApp?

Admira attributes Click-to-WhatsApp leads and connects them to CRMs like Kommo and Clientify. For other tools, ask to see WhatsApp leads matched to revenue in a demo before buying.

Can I run two attribution tools at once during a switch?

Yes, and it's a good idea for two to four weeks. Compare both against closed CRM revenue, not against each other.

Do Cometly alternatives include a Conversions API?

Many do, including Admira and Hyros, which send sales data back to ad platforms. Confirm which events (qualified lead, sale, revenue) each tool can send.
