The best social media tools with AI agents for 2027 are Hootsuite (Wisdom), Sprout Social (Trellis) and HubSpot's Breeze social media agent if you want an AI that analyzes and proposes posts for approval, and Buffer, Metricool or Ocoya if you want to connect your own AI assistant (Claude or ChatGPT) to schedule posts through MCP. Later, SocialBee and Publer offer AI writing assistants rather than agents, and Meta Business Suite stays the free option for Facebook and Instagram only. What counts as an "AI agent" in a social media tool?

Most social tools now say "AI". The label covers three very different things:

  1. AI writing assistant: you ask for a caption, it writes one. You still schedule it yourself.
  2. AI agent inside the tool: it reads your account data, analyzes performance, drafts posts or replies and recommends when to publish, usually with a human approval step.
  3. Agent connection (MCP or API): the tool lets an outside AI assistant draft and schedule posts in your queue, so you manage social from a chat window.

None of the tools we checked describe an agent that publishes on its own with no review. Hootsuite states that people remain in control of reviewing, approving and publishing. HubSpot says you review and approve every social post before it is published or scheduled. Buffer says you are always the last step. That is a good default.

Hootsuite: Wisdom, a social AI agent with approvals

Hootsuite's Wisdom replaces OwlyGPT. It analyzes trends, competitors and performance, summarizes reports and drafts posts, captions and campaign briefs. Hootsuite says people stay in control of reviewing, approving and publishing (Hootsuite Wisdom). Every plan includes Wisdom; Standard starts at $99/user/month billed annually, with a 14-day trial (Hootsuite plans).

Pros: the most complete in-app agent we found. Limits: cost per user is high for small teams.

Sprout Social: Trellis plus inbox and listening

Sprout calls Trellis an AI agent. It is on all plans with 100 credits per seat per month and provides inbox briefings, report summaries and drafted replies and post variations. Trellis Plus costs $28/seat/month annually for 1,000 credits. Essentials is $79/seat/month annually, with a 30-day trial (Sprout pricing).

Pros: strong for customer care and reporting. Limits: credits cap heavy AI use; no autonomous actions described.

HubSpot Breeze social media agent

HubSpot's agent suggests posts for Facebook, LinkedIn, X and Instagram based on your past performance and picks the best days and times. You review and approve every post. It is in beta, English only, and included in Marketing Hub Professional and Enterprise (HubSpot). Professional starts at $800/month with 3 seats plus a $3,000 onboarding fee (HubSpot pricing).

Pros: uses CRM context. Limits: only worth it if you use HubSpot for more than social.

Buffer: simple scheduling plus MCP for your own agent

Buffer's free plan covers 3 channels and 10 scheduled posts per channel. Paid plans start at $5 per channel per month, and the AI Assistant is on every plan (Buffer pricing). Its MCP connection lets Claude or ChatGPT draft posts, schedule them and manage your queue; posts land as drafts or scheduled items you can review (Buffer MCP).

Pros: cheap and easy. Limits: the agent is your outside assistant, so results depend on how you prompt it.

Metricool: low price, MCP, popular in Spanish-speaking markets

Metricool's free plan includes 1 brand, 20 posts a month and 5 AI credits. Starter shows from $20/month (about $25 on the higher listed price), and every plan lists Metricool MCP access, with limits that may apply (Metricool pricing).

Pros: analytics and ads reports included. Limits: small AI credit allowance.

Ocoya: workflows and MCP

Ocoya lists visual workflows to automate posting and replies, an MCP connection so Claude or ChatGPT can schedule posts, and credits for AI generation. Starter is $29/month for 5 channels, with a 7-day trial that requires a card (Ocoya pricing).

Pros: automation beyond scheduling. Limits: credits shared across AI, automations and exports.

Later: visual planning with an AI caption writer

Later's Starter is $18.75/month billed yearly with 5 AI credits; one credit makes 1 caption or 3 ideas (Later pricing). No agent is described.

Pros: great visual calendar. Limits: AI is a writing helper only.

SocialBee: AI Copilot for strategy

SocialBee's AI Copilot generates a social strategy with ready-to-use posts. Bootstrap is $29/month or $24/month yearly, with a 14-day trial (SocialBee pricing).

Pros: category-based queues. Limits: no agent or autonomous posting described.

Publer: cheapest per account

Publer has a free plan. Professional starts at $5/month for 1 account without AI Assist; Business starts at $10/month and adds unlimited AI Assist prompts (Publer help center).

Pros: low cost. Limits: writing assistant only.

Meta Business Suite: free for Facebook and Instagram

Meta Business Suite lets you create, schedule and manage Facebook and Instagram posts and stories on desktop and mobile at no cost (Meta Business Help Center). Meta has been adding AI features and began testing a Meta AI business assistant with advertisers in late 2025, but which features you see varies by account and region.

Pros: free and native. Limits: Meta platforms only.

When these tools are not enough

Scheduling tools report likes, reach and clicks. They do not tell you whether a post produced a lead that became a sale. If organic social drives WhatsApp chats or demo requests, you need tracked links and CRM data. Start with our guides on content and organic attribution and dark social attribution, and use consistent UTM tagging. That is where a tool like Admira fits: it ties traffic and leads to CRM revenue, but it does not publish posts. For paid social creative, see best AI ad generators.

FAQ

Can an AI agent post to social media without my approval?

None of the tools we checked describe it. Hootsuite, HubSpot and Buffer all describe a human review step before publishing.

What is the difference between an AI agent and an AI writing assistant?

A writing assistant produces text when you ask. An agent reads account data, analyzes it and proposes or prepares actions, such as drafts with suggested times.

What is MCP in a social media tool?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) lets an outside assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT connect to the tool and act in it, for example adding posts to your queue.

Which tool is cheapest with AI?

Buffer includes its AI Assistant even on the free plan. Publer's AI Assist starts on its $10/month Business plan.

Is Meta Business Suite enough?

For Facebook and Instagram only, often yes. If you also post to LinkedIn, TikTok or X, you need a third-party scheduler.

Do AI agents improve results?

They save time on drafting and analysis. Whether posts drive revenue still has to be measured with tracked links and CRM data.
