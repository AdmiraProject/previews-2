The best AI ad generator depends on the job: AdCreative.ai and Pencil for high volumes of static and display ads, Creatify and Arcads for AI avatar and UGC-style video, Canva for teams that want AI inside a general design tool, Meta Advantage+ creative and Google's Performance Max asset generation for free in-platform variations, and Admira if you need creative generation next to attribution that shows which ads produced CRM revenue. Prices range from free to about $1,000 a month, and several vendors change plans often, so every price below was checked in November 2026 and marked where it is not published.

This comparison covers what each tool does, verified pricing, who it fits, and a simple way to pick one based on your volume and how you measure results.

AdCreative.ai: best for volume of static ads

AdCreative.ai generates banners and social images from your brand assets and gives each creative a predicted performance score. Its homepage listed Starter at $39/month with 20 credits and one brand, Professional at $249/month and Ultimate at $999/month, with a 7-day free trial (checked Nov 2026 on adcreative.ai). Credits limit how many creatives you can download, so model the cost per usable ad, not the plan price.

Watch for: scores are predictions, not results. Judge winners in your own account.

Creatify: best for AI avatar product videos

Creatify turns a product URL or script into video ads with AI actors. Its pricing page lists a free plan with 10 credits a month (up to 2 video ads, with watermark), Standard at $15/month, Pro at $55/month, Max at $95/month and a Business team plan at $195/month, with about 30% off on annual billing. Credits on paid plans expire after two months.

Watch for: credit cost varies by model and output, and the tool shows the cost before each generation.

Arcads: best for UGC-style actor videos

Arcads focuses on UGC-style video with a library it describes as more than 1,000 AI actors, plus video models and translation into more than 30 languages, according to its homepage. Pricing is not published on the site, so you see it after signing up.

Watch for: UGC-style ads must not pose as real customer testimonials. See our guide to AI UGC video ads for disclosure rules.

Pencil: best for small teams that want generation plus tracking

Pencil sells AI agents for text, images, video and ad creatives, with performance scores and ad launching on major platforms. Its pricing page lists Core at $14/month ($11 billed yearly) with 50 generations, Growth at $55/month ($44 yearly) with 250 generations, and a custom Pro plan with unlimited generations.

Watch for: generation caps; plan for how many usable assets you need per month.

Canva: best when AI is one part of a design workflow

Canva bundles AI image, text and video features into a general design tool, which suits teams that already design everything there. There is a free plan. Paid plan prices vary by country and billing period, and we could not load Canva's pricing page during our check, so confirm on canva.com/pricing.

Watch for: Canva is a design tool first. It does not test ads or read ad results for you.

Meta Advantage+ creative and Google asset generation: best free starting point

Both platforms build generation into the ad tools you already use, at no extra charge beyond media spend. Meta's generative AI features include background generation, image expansion, text variations and image-to-video, and ads made with them can carry Meta's "AI info" label. Google's Performance Max asset generation suggests text, image and video assets from your website, and you review them before they run.

Watch for: each platform optimizes for its own reported conversions. If those conversions are cheap leads, the AI will make more ads that bring cheap leads. Read how to measure Advantage+ campaigns before trusting the reported lift.

Admira: best when you need to know which creative made revenue

Admira is a marketing attribution platform first. Its Growth plan ($399/month, up to 30,000 unique visitors and 6 seats) includes AI Ads, an AI ad creative generator, alongside attribution that ties Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and Click-to-WhatsApp leads to revenue in HubSpot, Salesforce, Kommo, Clientify or ActiveCampaign. That closes the loop that most generators leave open: you see which ad produced sales, then generate more like it. Honest limits: Admira is a young brand with few public reviews, it is not an avatar video studio, and for a pure Shopify store an ecommerce tool may fit better. Plan details are on the pricing page.

How do you choose an AI ad generator?

  1. Count the ads you need per month by format (static, video, UGC-style).
  2. Start free in-platform with Meta and Google tools to learn what variation volume your budget can test.
  3. Add a specialist only for the format the platforms do poorly for you, such as avatar video.
  4. Calculate cost per usable ad: plan price divided by ads that pass brand review.
  5. Decide how you will judge winners. If the answer is "CTR in Ads Manager", fix measurement first with creative-level attribution.

Example (hypothetical numbers): an agency needs 60 static ads and 20 short videos a month for three clients. A $55/month static plan yields about 45 usable statics after review, so cost per usable static is about $1.22. A $95/month video plan yields 16 usable videos, about $5.94 each. Total tooling is $150 a month. If one winning creative lowers a client's cost per sale from $80 to $70 on 50 sales, it saves $500 a month, more than three times the tooling cost. If you cannot measure cost per sale by creative, you cannot see that saving at all.

Who this is for, and who it isn't

  • For: performance teams and agencies that ship new creative weekly and want to cut production cost.
  • Not for: brands that run a few campaigns a year; a designer and the free in-platform tools are enough.
  • Not for: anyone planning to use AI to fake reviews or testimonials. That breaks platform rules and, in the US, the FTC rule effective October 21, 2024.

FAQ

What is the best free AI ad generator?

Meta's generative AI features in Ads Manager and Google's Performance Max asset generation cost nothing extra. Creatify also has a free plan with watermarked exports.

Which AI tool is best for UGC video ads?

Creatify and Arcads both focus on AI actor video. Creatify publishes prices from $15/month; Arcads does not publish prices on its site.

Are AI ad generator performance scores reliable?

Treat them as a pre-filter. Only results in your own account, ideally measured to qualified leads or sales, show what works.

Do I need to label ads made with AI generators?

Often, yes. TikTok requires its AI-generated content disclaimer for fully or significantly AI-made media, and Meta labels qualifying ads with "AI info".

Can one tool cover static, video and measurement?

Rarely well. Most teams combine a generator with an attribution tool that ties creative to revenue.

How often do these prices change?

Often. Vendors in this category adjust plans and credit limits regularly, so check the vendor's page on the day you buy.
