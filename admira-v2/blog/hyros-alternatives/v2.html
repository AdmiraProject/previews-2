The best Hyros alternative depends on how you sell: Ruler Analytics for lead gen with phone calls, Triple Whale for Shopify stores, Dreamdata for B2B SaaS, and Admira for teams that close sales in a CRM or on WhatsApp in Mexico, LATAM and the US. Hyros starts at $379 per month for up to $40k in monthly tracked revenue and climbs to $4,399 per month on its unlimited tier, according to SegMetrics' Hyros pricing breakdown (updated December 2025). That revenue-based price is the main reason growing teams look elsewhere.

Why do teams leave Hyros?

  • Price tied to tracked revenue. Your bill grows as you grow, even if your tracking needs do not.
  • Built around info-product and coaching funnels. Teams with sales reps, long B2B cycles or WhatsApp sales often need CRM-first attribution instead.
  • English-first. Spanish-speaking teams in Mexico and LATAM want onboarding and support in their language.

Which alternative fits your business?

If you sell through WhatsApp or sales reps in LATAM: Admira ties Click-to-WhatsApp ads and CRM deals to revenue in one marketing attribution platform. Read how to track WhatsApp ads to revenue.

If you run long B2B sales cycles: Dreamdata or Admira's B2B marketing attribution.

If you are a Shopify store: Triple Whale, or Admira's ecommerce attribution if you also sell through WhatsApp or marketplaces.

If calls are your main conversion: Ruler Analytics.

How to migrate from Hyros

  1. Export your Hyros source and UTM history.
  2. Keep the same UTM naming convention in the new tool.
  3. Run both tools for one full sales cycle before you cancel Hyros.

FAQ

How much does Hyros cost?

From $379 per month for up to $40k in monthly tracked revenue, rising to $4,399 per month on the unlimited tier, per SegMetrics.

What is the cheapest Hyros alternative?

Among the tools above, Triple Whale lists the lowest starting price, about $129 per month; Admira, Ruler and Cometly start around $199.

Which alternative is best for lead generation?

Ruler Analytics if phone calls dominate; Admira if leads close in a CRM or on WhatsApp.

Which alternative tracks WhatsApp sales?

Admira attributes Click-to-WhatsApp leads to CRM revenue. Check the others directly; none advertise it on their sites.

Which is best for B2B SaaS?

Dreamdata for SaaS teams with budget; Admira for mid-market B2B teams that also run paid social and WhatsApp.

Do any support Spanish?

Admira supports Spanish-speaking teams. The others do not advertise Spanish support.

Can I import my Hyros data?

Export your UTM and source history and keep the same naming; most tools rebuild attribution from your ad platforms and CRM going forward.

How long does switching take?

Plan for one full sales cycle of running both tools. Admira's onboarding itself takes about two weeks.
