The best AgencyAnalytics alternatives in 2026 are DashThis and Swydo for simpler client dashboards, Databox for KPI tracking with a free plan, Whatagraph for large agencies that want a managed data layer, Looker Studio for free custom builds, Reportei for agencies that want social, paid and CRM data in one report with a Spanish interface, and Admira when clients need ad spend tied to CRM revenue instead of platform metrics. The right pick depends on why you are leaving: cost at your client count, design flexibility, or proof that campaigns produce sales.

AgencyAnalytics is a solid product, so it helps to be clear about what it offers before comparing. Its pricing page (checked November 2026) lists a Core plan at $20 per client per month billed annually, a 14-day trial with no credit card, and volume pricing for agencies with more than 25 clients. Its features page says it pulls data from 90+ marketing platforms and includes white-label reports, a branded client portal, daily rank tracking, alerts and AI tools. This guide compares the alternatives on the reasons agencies actually switch.

Why do agencies look for AgencyAnalytics alternatives?

Most agencies that leave AgencyAnalytics are not unhappy with the dashboards. They hit one of four limits.

  • Cost grows with every client. Per-client pricing is fair for a few large retainers. It hurts when you manage many small accounts. Example: 40 clients x $20 = $800 per month on annual billing, before any volume discount.
  • Clients want sales, not clicks. A report full of impressions, CTR and platform conversions does not answer "how many customers did this bring us?" Platform numbers also overlap, which we explain in why platform ROAS inflates results.
  • Custom data needs. Some agencies want to blend sources, build their own calculated metrics or push data to a warehouse.
  • Regional fit. Agencies in Mexico and LATAM often need Spanish support and a way to report on leads that arrive through WhatsApp.

If none of these apply to you, staying put is usually cheaper than migrating.

Which AgencyAnalytics alternative fits each case?

DashThis: the simplest switch

DashThis focuses on clean dashboards with preset templates. Pricing is per dashboard: Individual covers 3 dashboards, Professional 10, Business 25 and Standard starts at 50. If each client gets one dashboard, the math is easy to predict. It does not include SEO tools such as rank tracking, so SEO-heavy agencies may miss that part of AgencyAnalytics.

Swydo: the budget option for many clients

Swydo has one plan with all features, unlimited clients, users and reports, and charges by data source. The first 10 data sources are included, then each extra source from 11 to 100 costs €4.50. Agencies with lots of small accounts often pay less than with per-client pricing, but you should count your data sources first, because a client with Google Ads, Meta, GA4 and Search Console uses four.

Databox: KPIs and goals over long reports

Databox is built around metrics, goals and alerts rather than long PDF reports. It has a free plan with 3 data sources, which makes it easy to test. When we checked, its pricing page showed different agency figures in the table and in the FAQ ($79 vs $99 per month), so confirm the current number with sales.

Whatagraph: for larger agencies

Whatagraph prices from €699 per month on annual billing, with credits per connected data account and unlimited users and reports. It targets agencies that want one place to manage data for many accounts. It is overkill for a small team, and its page lists only a demo, not a free trial.

Looker Studio: free and flexible, but you build it

Google's Looker Studio is free to build and share reports, and it gives you full design control. The cost is time: you build templates, maintain connectors and fix broken charts. Native connectors cover Google products; Meta, TikTok and others usually need a paid connector such as Supermetrics (from $44 per month billed yearly, checked November 2026). We cover the trade-offs in Looker Studio limits and alternatives and in our comparison of Supermetrics alternatives.

Reportei: social and paid media in one place

Reportei describes itself as a tool for digital marketing reports and dashboards covering paid traffic, social media, SEO and sales. Its site lists more than 40 integrations, including Instagram, TikTok, Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4, HubSpot, Kommo and Shopify, and it is available in English, Portuguese and Spanish. It offers a 3-day free trial. We could not confirm current plan prices on the pages we checked, so ask for a quote before comparing.

Admira: when the client asks "how many sales?"

Admira is a different category. It is a marketing attribution platform that connects Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and Click-to-WhatsApp leads to revenue in the client's CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Kommo, Clientify, ActiveCampaign and others). Reports show cost per lead, cost per sale and revenue by campaign, not only clicks. It can also send CRM events back to ad platforms through a Conversion API. Pricing starts at $199 per month per account, and its agency partner program pays 50% of MRR for each referred client. The honest limits: it is a young brand with few public reviews, it has no SEO rank tracking, and it is not a replacement for a cheap white-label dashboard for every small client.

When should you stay with AgencyAnalytics?

Switching is not always the right move. Stay if:

  • Your agency sells SEO and uses the rank tracker, site audits and backlink data every week.
  • You manage fewer than about 15 clients, so per-client pricing is still moderate.
  • Clients are happy with channel metrics and do not ask for revenue or cost per sale.
  • Your team has templates that work and no time to rebuild them.

This guide is not for in-house teams with a single brand. They usually need a marketing dashboard or attribution tool, not agency reporting software.

How do you switch reporting tools without breaking client reports?

  1. List what each client actually reads. Export your current report templates and mark the metrics clients mention in calls.
  2. Count clients, dashboards and data sources. This is what each vendor charges for, so your estimate depends on it.
  3. Run a two-week trial with 2 or 3 real clients. Pick one simple account and one messy account.
  4. Compare totals. Spend, clicks and conversions should match the ad platforms for the same date range and time zone.
  5. Rebuild templates once, then clone them across clients.
  6. Send both reports for one cycle so clients see the new format next to the old one.
  7. Cancel the old tool only after the first full month runs cleanly.

FAQ

How much does AgencyAnalytics cost in 2026?

Its pricing page lists $20 per client per month on annual billing for the Core plan, with volume pricing for 25+ clients on request. There is a 14-day free trial with no credit card.

What is the cheapest AgencyAnalytics alternative?

Looker Studio is free, but you pay in setup time and connector fees. Among paid tools, Swydo and DashThis have the lowest entry prices we found.

Which alternative has a free plan?

Databox has a free plan with 3 data sources, and Looker Studio is free to use. Most others offer a 14-day trial instead.

Is there an AgencyAnalytics alternative with white label?

Yes. DashThis, Swydo, Databox and Whatagraph all offer branded reports in some form. Check which plan includes custom domains, since that is often an upper-tier feature.

Can these tools show revenue from ads?

Most show what ad platforms and analytics report. To show closed sales, you need CRM data joined to ad clicks, which is what attribution tools like Admira do.

Do I need a different tool for WhatsApp leads?

Standard reporting tools do not see what happens inside WhatsApp conversations. Tracking Click-to-WhatsApp leads to a sale requires CRM integration and attribution, covered in our WhatsApp ads attribution guide.
