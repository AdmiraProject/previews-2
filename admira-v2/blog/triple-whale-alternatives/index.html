The strongest Triple Whale alternatives in 2026 are Northbeam for DTC brands that want deeper multi-touch attribution, Polar Analytics for a Snowflake-backed ecommerce BI stack, Lifetimely (by AMP) for profit and LTV on a budget, Wicked Reports for cohort and subscription reporting, Hyros or Cometly for ad tracking with sales sent back to Meta and Google, and Admira for brands whose sales close through WhatsApp, a sales team or a CRM instead of a checkout page. If you're a pure Shopify store and Triple Whale already works, it may still be the best fit.

Triple Whale offers a free plan and paid plans called Foundation, Automate and Enterprise. Paid prices aren't published; they depend on your annual GMV (order revenue over the last 365 days) and the package you pick, on 12-month subscriptions with unlimited users (per a third-party pricing review checked on November 4, 2026, Kanal). That GMV link is the most common reason brands start comparing.

Why do brands look for Triple Whale alternatives?

  • Price grows with revenue. Because pricing is tied to GMV, your bill rises as you grow, even if your ad strategy stays the same.
  • Annual commitment. Paid plans run on 12-month subscriptions.
  • Attribution depth. Some brands want independent multi-touch models, view-through or incrementality options beyond the core dashboards.
  • Not every sale is a Shopify order. Brands that sell high-ticket products through WhatsApp, showrooms or sales reps need CRM revenue, not just checkout data.

Which Triple Whale alternative is right for you?

Northbeam: for deeper multi-touch attribution

Northbeam lists independent first-party MTA, customizable dashboards, custom attribution and view-through attribution on Starter, and adds a dedicated media strategist on Professional ($3,500/month, for brands spending up to $500K a month on ads). Incrementality is an add-on on Professional; MMM+ and incrementality on Enterprise (Northbeam pricing). Limits: higher entry cost, and Professional and Enterprise are annual terms. We compare all three in Triple Whale vs Northbeam vs Admira.

Polar Analytics: for a full ecommerce data stack

Polar's plans include a dedicated Snowflake database, an ecommerce semantic layer, a first-party pixel with multi-touch attribution, unlimited users and unlimited historical data, plus incrementality testing on its Custom plan (Polar pricing). Limits: prices aren't shown on the page; you select GMV and book a demo.

Lifetimely: for profit and LTV on a budget

Lifetimely, now part of AMP, has a free plan up to 50 orders per month and paid plans from $149/month for 3,000 orders, with daily P&L, predictive LTV and custom dashboards (AMP pricing). Limits: it's a profit analytics tool first, not a multi-touch ad attribution platform.

Wicked Reports: for subscription and repeat purchase

Wicked Reports starts at $499/month for brands up to $2.5M in annual revenue and includes cohort and LTV reports with lifetime lookback; Meta CAPI (Advanced Signal) is included on Maximize ($999) or +$199/month on lower plans (Wicked Reports pricing).

Hyros and Cometly: for paid-media tracking

Both focus on ad tracking and sending conversions back to the ad platforms. Neither publishes prices: Hyros quotes by tracked revenue, Cometly by pageviews. See Cometly alternatives and Hyros alternatives.

Admira: when the sale doesn't happen at checkout

Some brands advertise like ecommerce but sell like a sales team: furniture, solar, cars, medical aesthetics, education. Leads arrive through Click-to-WhatsApp ads or forms and close days later in Kommo, HubSpot or Salesforce. Admira is built for that: it ties ad leads to CRM revenue and sends qualified events back through the Conversions API. Public pricing starts at $199/month. Limits: for a pure Shopify store with online checkout, an ecommerce tool like Triple Whale may fit better; Admira is also a young brand with few public reviews. Our ecommerce attribution page explains where it fits.

How do you pick the right alternative?

  1. Write down your revenue source. Shopify orders only? Or also WhatsApp, phone, showroom or wholesale?
  2. Decide your main question. "Which ad drove this order?" points to attribution tools. "Am I profitable after COGS and shipping?" points to profit tools like Lifetimely.
  3. Check pricing on your own numbers. Collect annual GMV, orders per month, pageviews and ad spend, and get quotes on the same inputs.
  4. Check contract terms. Ask for monthly options and what happens when you cross a GMV or order tier.
  5. Validate against reality. Run the new tool alongside your current one for a month and compare both against Shopify revenue and blended MER (how to use MER as a sanity check).

When should you stay with Triple Whale?

Stay if you're a Shopify-first brand, your team uses the dashboards daily, and the quoted GMV tier is reasonable. The free plan is also enough for small stores that mainly need a single view of performance. Switching costs time, and a new tool needs a few weeks before its numbers are trusted.

This guide is not for: B2B or SaaS companies or brands looking mainly for MMM (see Northbeam alternatives, which covers MMM-capable tools). For a pricing overview across the category, read our attribution software pricing guide.

FAQ

What is the best alternative to Triple Whale?

For deeper DTC attribution, Northbeam; for an ecommerce data warehouse, Polar Analytics; for profit and LTV on a budget, Lifetimely; for brands selling through WhatsApp or a sales team, Admira.

How much does Triple Whale cost in 2026?

Triple Whale has a free plan. Paid plans (Foundation, Automate, Enterprise) aren't published and are priced by annual GMV and package on 12-month subscriptions, according to a pricing review checked on November 4, 2026.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Triple Whale?

Lifetimely starts free and has paid plans from $149/month; Admira starts at $199/month for lead-based sales. Whether it's cheaper depends on your Triple Whale GMV quote.

Is Northbeam better than Triple Whale?

Neither is better for everyone. Northbeam puts more emphasis on independent MTA and media strategy support at a higher price; Triple Whale is Shopify-native with a free entry point.

Does any Triple Whale alternative work for WhatsApp sales?

Admira attributes Click-to-WhatsApp leads and matches them to revenue in CRMs like Kommo and Clientify. Most ecommerce tools focus on checkout orders.

Can I use Triple Whale and another tool together?

Yes. Some brands keep Triple Whale's free plan for daily dashboards and add a profit or CRM-based tool for the questions it doesn't answer.
