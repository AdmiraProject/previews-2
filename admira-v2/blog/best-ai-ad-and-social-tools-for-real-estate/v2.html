The best AI ad and social tools for real estate in 2027 are Canva, Creatify and AdCreative.ai to turn listings into ads and videos, Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads to launch them inside each platform's housing rules, Metricool, Later or Buffer to schedule listing posts, and Admira to see which ads became site visits and sales. The housing rules matter more than the AI: in the US and Canada, both Meta and Google block age, gender and ZIP-code targeting for housing ads, so AI targeting tricks that work for ecommerce do not apply. We checked prices and policies on each vendor's page in November 2026 for 2027 planning.

Admira publishes this post and appears in the creation and measurement sections, with its limits. This is marketing guidance, not legal advice.

How did we choose these tools?

We sorted tools by the four jobs a brokerage or developer's marketing team does: (a) create listing ads and videos, (b) launch and optimize ads, (c) schedule and optimize social posts, and (d) measure which ads led to visits and closed sales. For each tool we read the vendor's own pricing and feature pages in November 2026, noted the starting price, whether the AI takes actions (automation) or drafts content you publish (writing helper), and the key limit for housing ads. We used no reviews or rankings we could not verify.

What do Meta and Google allow for real estate ads?

RuleMeta (Housing special ad category)Google (housing, personalized ads)
Where it appliesUS-based advertisers and ads reaching the US, Canada or Europe; businesses elsewhere may opt in or outAds targeting the US and Canada
AgeFixed at 18 to 65+Cannot target by age
GenderCannot be selectedCannot target by gender
LocationMinimum 15-mile (25 km) radius in the US and Canada, 15 km in Europe; no ZIP codes or location exclusionsNo ZIP codes; radius from 1 km, city and country allowed; Canada allows the first 3 postal-code characters
OtherNo lookalike audiences, no detailed targeting exclusions, interests only from an approved listNo parental or marital status targeting

Sources: Meta's special ad category documentation and Google's housing in personalized advertising policy, both checked November 2026. Separately, the US Fair Housing Act regulations cover the wording of housing ads, not just the targeting: ads may not indicate a preference or limitation based on protected classes such as race, religion, sex, disability, familial status or national origin (24 CFR 100.75). AI copy tools can produce lines like "perfect for young professionals", so review every draft.

Example for Latin America: a developer in Tulum selling only to Mexican buyers is not automatically under Meta's housing restrictions, but the same developer running ads to buyers in Texas or Canada is.

Which AI tools turn listings into ads and videos?

Canva

Canva is a simple place to build listing graphics. Brand kits keep every listing on brand, and the AI tools draft layouts and copy. It is a helper: you still decide what goes out.

Creatify

Creatify turns a URL into short video ads with AI actors. Plans start free with 10 credits and watermarks, then $10/month billed yearly for Standard and $38/month yearly for Pro (Creatify pricing). It is designed around product links, so test it with a listing page before buying. Avoid AI actors posing as past buyers.

AdCreative.ai

AdCreative.ai produces static variations and scores them before launch; plans start at $39/month billed monthly or $20/month yearly, with a 7-day trial (AdCreative.ai). Useful when you advertise many units and need fresh images each week.

Admira AI Ads

Admira's site says AI Ads create static, video and UGC-style ads and that its attribution shows which creatives drive revenue (AI ad creative generator). It is part of the Growth plan at $399/month.

Which tools launch and optimize real estate ads?

Meta Ads Manager

Meta reaches buyers before they search, and Click-to-WhatsApp ads move them straight into a chat (see our Click-to-WhatsApp ads guide). Declare the Housing category whenever it applies, and send qualified-lead and visit events back through the Conversions API, so Meta's automation learns from buyers, not form fillers.

Google Ads

Search captures buyers who already know the area or the project name. Performance Max and automatically created assets save time, but review generated text against fair-housing wording rules.

Which tools schedule and optimize listing posts?

Metricool has a free plan (1 brand, 20 scheduled posts a month) and Starter from $20/month yearly or $25 monthly, with an AI assistant, best times and reporting for Facebook, Google and TikTok ads (Metricool pricing). Later starts at $18.75/month billed yearly with an AI caption writer and best time to post (Later pricing). Buffer is free for 3 channels and $5 per channel/month after that, with approvals on the Team plan (Buffer pricing). All three draft captions with AI; none decides what to publish for you.

How do you measure which ads sold homes?

Real estate cycles are long, so platform metrics like cost per lead mislead. Track each lead from ad to visit to reservation to sale. Example: cost per sale for a development

Example (hypothetical numbers): a developer spends $10,000/month on Meta and Google for a 60-unit project. It gets 400 leads, 90% through WhatsApp. Sales qualifies 120, 60 visit the showroom and 4 reserve a unit. Cost per lead is $25, cost per visit is about $167 and cost per sale is $2,500. When results are split by creative, one walkthrough video drove 1 of every 2 visits at half the budget, so the team moves spend to that video and pauses static carousels with low visit rates.

How do you set up the stack in 30 days?

  1. Decide whether the Housing category applies to each campaign, based on where you advertise.
  2. Write a short copy checklist: no phrases about who the home is "for", accurate prices and delivery dates.
  3. Produce 10 to 15 creatives per project with one creation tool and review every draft.
  4. Launch on Meta and Google with fixed test budgets.
  5. Schedule listing posts weekly with one social tool.
  6. Track leads to visits and sales in your CRM and move budget monthly.

Who this is for, and who it isn't for

This fits developers, brokerages and agents who advertise listings or projects on Meta and Google. It is less useful if most of your business comes from portals, referrals or a single agent's network, where portal tools and relationship tracking matter more than ad automation.

FAQ

Do Meta housing restrictions apply in Mexico?

Meta applies them to US-based advertisers and ads reaching the US, Canada or Europe; businesses elsewhere may opt in or out. If you advertise to US buyers, they apply.

Can I use lookalike audiences for real estate ads?

Not under Meta's Housing category, where lookalike audiences are unavailable.

Can AI write my listing ads?

Yes, as a draft. A person should check prices, dates and fair-housing wording before anything goes live.

What is the minimum radius for housing ads?

Meta requires at least 15 miles (25 km) in the US and Canada and 15 km in Europe. Google allows radius targeting from 1 km but bans ZIP codes.

Which social tool is best for real estate?

Metricool for multi-network posting with ad reporting, Later for Instagram-first agents and Buffer for the lowest cost.

How do I know which ad sold a unit?

Connect each lead's source to your CRM and track it through visit and sale. Platform cost per lead alone cannot tell you.
