Choose Admira if your sales close in a CRM or on WhatsApp (HubSpot, Salesforce, Kommo, Clientify) and you want flat, published pricing from $199/month; choose Hyros if you sell courses, coaching or Shopify and Stripe funnels and want revenue-based pricing with a setup team and call tracking built for booked-call offers. Both track first-party data and send conversions back to Meta and Google server-side. They differ most in how they charge, which sales stack they plug into, and how they handle WhatsApp.

Disclosure: Admira publishes this blog, and the author is Admira's founder. To keep it fair, every Hyros fact below comes from Hyros's own pages, checked in November 2026, and we list where Hyros is the better pick.

Which one costs less for a lead-gen business?

For high-ticket lead-gen with modest traffic, visitor pricing usually costs less than revenue pricing, because a single closed deal can be worth thousands of dollars while traffic stays small. For a low-ticket store with lots of traffic and thin revenue, the opposite can be true.

Example (hypothetical numbers): a Mexican real estate developer runs Meta and Click-to-WhatsApp ads.

Whether Hyros would count every property sale as tracked revenue depends on how the sale is recorded, so ask Hyros for a quote using your real CRM revenue. For a deeper breakdown of seat limits and onboarding fees across vendors, see our attribution software pricing guide.

Which one handles WhatsApp and CRM sales better?

This is where the two tools differ most. Admira was built for teams where the sale happens after a conversation: a Click-to-WhatsApp chat in Kommo or Clientify, or a deal that moves through stages in HubSpot or Salesforce. It ties the ad click to the lead and then to the closed revenue, and sends the CRM stage back to Meta so the algorithm learns from sales, not just messages. Our WhatsApp ads attribution page shows the flow.

Hyros's integration list centers on checkout and funnel tools: Shopify, WooCommerce, Stripe, GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels and Kajabi. We did not find a native Kommo or Clientify connector on its pages. Meta's own Conversions API for Business Messaging docs explain what any tool needs for WhatsApp: the ctwa_clid click ID from the first message and events like LeadSubmitted, QualifiedLead or Purchase.

Where is Hyros the better choice?

  • Booked-call offers. Hyros reports call quality and close rates by sales rep, and overlays CallRail. Admira does not advertise call tracking.
  • Profit per ad. Hyros tracks COGS so you can see profit, not just revenue, per ad.
  • Funnel builders and Shopify. Native ClickFunnels, Kajabi, Shopify and Stripe connections fit info-product and DTC funnels. Admira states that a pure Shopify store with online checkout may be better served by an ecommerce tool.
  • Long track record. Hyros says it serves 5,000+ brands. Admira is a young brand with few public reviews, so you have less third-party proof to lean on.
  • Refund promise. Hyros states a 90-day refund if results are not achieved. Admira does not advertise a guarantee.

Where is Admira the better choice?

  • WhatsApp and LATAM CRMs. Native Kommo, Clientify and WhatsApp Business Platform support, with Spanish support.
  • Predictable pricing. Prices are on the pricing page and do not rise when your revenue grows.
  • Teams and reporting. Growth includes 6 seats, a report builder, unlimited dashboards and shared reports; Custom adds unlimited seats and user roles.
  • Privacy defaults. Cookieless tracking by default, data on AWS in the US, GDPR and CCPA compliance.
  • Published customer results. On the customers page, KW (real estate) reports 25% faster closing cycles, and lender Apurata raised loan completion 35% after finding the drop-off stage.

How do you test both before you commit? (5 steps)

  1. Pick one campaign with at least 30 days of closed revenue in your CRM or payment processor.
  2. Export the truth. List every closed deal or payment from that period, with date and amount.
  3. Ask each vendor to match it. How many of those sales does each tool tie to an ad, campaign and creative?
  4. Check the return path. Confirm which CRM events get sent back to Meta and Google, and that events are deduplicated.
  5. Price your next 12 months. Quote both at today's traffic and revenue, then at 2x revenue. Our 15 questions to ask attribution vendors help structure the calls.

Who this comparison is for, and who it isn't

For: marketing leads and founders at lead-gen, B2B, real estate, education or lending companies deciding between the two, and agencies picking a tool for several clients.

Not for: teams that need media mix modeling (neither tool is an MMM consultancy; see MTA vs MMM), or businesses that only want a free ad-platform dashboard. If you also weigh Cometly, read Hyros vs Cometly.

FAQ

Is Admira cheaper than Hyros?

For high-ticket lead-gen with modest traffic, usually yes, because Admira prices by visitors ($199 to $1,500/month) and Hyros by tracked revenue. For high-traffic, low-revenue sites, compare quotes; Hyros's published entry is $230/month on annual billing.

Does Hyros integrate with Kommo or WhatsApp?

We did not find a native Kommo or Clientify connector on Hyros's pages. Ask Hyros to demo Click-to-WhatsApp attribution with Meta's ctwa_clid before you buy.

Does Admira track phone calls like Hyros?

Call tracking is not a headline Admira feature. If phone-booked sales are your main channel, Hyros's call reporting is the stronger fit.

How long does setup take?

Admira's technical setup takes minutes and full onboarding with dashboards and team training takes about two weeks. Hyros says setup takes about 15 minutes for most businesses, with its setup team included.

Which is better for agencies?

Admira's partner program pays 50% of MRR per referred client; Hyros advertises 40% recurring commission. Pick the one that fits most of your clients' sales stacks.

Can I switch from Hyros to Admira?

Yes. Connect your ad accounts and CRM in Admira, run both in parallel for a few weeks, and compare matched revenue before you cancel. Historical touchpoints stay in Hyros unless you export them.
