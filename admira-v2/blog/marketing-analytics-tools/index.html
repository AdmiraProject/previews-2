The marketing analytics tools worth using in 2026 depend on the job: Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity for website behavior, Mixpanel for product events, the native Meta and Google Ads reports for in-platform optimization, Supermetrics for moving ad data into sheets or BI, Looker Studio or Power BI for dashboards, and an attribution platform such as Admira to connect ad spend with CRM revenue. Most teams need one tool per job, not one tool for everything, and several of the best options are free.

Below we group the tools by the question each one answers, list prices we checked on vendor sites in November 2026, and show what a sensible stack costs at different stages. If you want the concepts first, read our guide on what marketing analytics is.

Web analytics: GA4 and Microsoft Clarity

GA4 is the default for traffic sources, landing pages and on-site conversions. It is free and integrates with Google Ads. Two details matter: standard properties can keep user-level and event data for 2 or 14 months, a setting that affects explorations and funnel reports, and GA4 only sees what happens on your site. Microsoft Clarity complements it with heatmaps and session recordings, and its site describes it as "free forever" with no traffic limits. Use Clarity to see why a landing page does not convert.

Product analytics: Mixpanel

Mixpanel fits SaaS and apps that need funnels, retention and cohort analysis by event. Its free plan covers up to 1 million events per month. It is less useful for lead-gen companies whose conversion happens in a sales call or a WhatsApp chat.

Ad platform analytics: Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads

The native reports are the best place to optimize creatives, audiences, bids and placements. They are free and detailed. The problem appears when you add them up: each platform uses its own attribution window and claims its own conversions, so totals overlap. Read our post on attribution windows in Meta, Google and GA4 before comparing them.

Data movement and BI: Supermetrics, Looker Studio, Power BI, Databox

These tools put all channels in one view. Supermetrics pulls data from ad platforms into Google Sheets, Looker Studio, Excel or a warehouse; plans start at $44 per month billed yearly. Looker Studio is free and flexible. Power BI suits companies that already use Microsoft 365; Pro costs $14 per user per month billed yearly. Databox is the lightest option for KPI tracking. All of them show the data you feed them. None of them decides which platform's conversions are real. Compare options in our Supermetrics alternatives guide.

Attribution and revenue: CRM reports and attribution platforms

This is the layer that connects marketing to money. CRMs offer some of it: HubSpot's revenue attribution reports are available on Marketing Hub Enterprise, according to its knowledge base, and we list other gaps in HubSpot attribution limits. A dedicated tool goes further. Admira ties Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and Click-to-WhatsApp leads to revenue in HubSpot, Salesforce, Kommo, Clientify or ActiveCampaign, shows cost per sale and ROAS by campaign, and sends CRM events back to ad platforms through a Conversion API. How do you choose the right marketing analytics tools?

  1. Write the five questions above and mark which ones you cannot answer today.
  2. Use free tools first for web analytics and in-platform reporting. GA4, Clarity and native ad reports cover a lot.
  3. Decide where revenue lives. If sales close in a CRM or WhatsApp, an attribution layer matters more than a prettier dashboard.
  4. Check integrations. Confirm the tool connects to your actual CRM and ad accounts, not just the popular ones.
  5. Count users and data sources, because that is how most tools charge.
  6. Run a trial on real data and compare totals with the source platforms.
  7. Assign an owner. A tool nobody maintains becomes a dashboard nobody trusts.

Who should not add more analytics tools?

  • Teams with broken tracking. Fix UTMs, form fields and conversion events first, or every tool shows bad data.
  • Teams with one channel and online checkout, where GA4 and the ad platform already match closely.
  • Pure Shopify stores, which usually fit better with ecommerce-first tools than with lead-gen attribution.
  • Companies that need marketing mix modeling. That is a statistical project, not a dashboard purchase.

FAQ

What is the best free marketing analytics tool?

GA4 for website traffic and conversions, plus Microsoft Clarity for heatmaps and recordings. Looker Studio is the best free dashboard option for Google data.

Do I need a BI tool for marketing analytics?

Not always. Small teams can work with GA4, native ad reports and a simple dashboard. BI tools like Power BI help when you combine marketing with finance or sales data.

What is the difference between Supermetrics and an attribution tool?

Supermetrics moves data from platforms into reports. An attribution tool joins ad clicks to leads and sales, so it can show which campaigns produced revenue.

Which tool shows revenue by campaign?

A CRM with attribution reports or a dedicated attribution platform. Ad platforms show revenue only for conversions they track themselves.

Can one tool do all of marketing analytics?

Rarely well. Most teams combine free web and ad analytics with one paid layer for dashboards or attribution.

How much should I spend on marketing analytics tools?

There is no fixed rule. Compare the tool cost with the ad spend it helps you allocate and with the hours it saves on manual reporting.
