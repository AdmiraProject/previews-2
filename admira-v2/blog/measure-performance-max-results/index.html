How to Measure Performance Max Real Results

Measure Performance Max real results in 3 steps: CRM-based conversions, channel and search terms reports, and an uplift experiment against CRM revenue.

To measure Performance Max real results, do three things: make sure it optimizes toward conversions that mean revenue (CRM-qualified leads or sales, with values), use Google's own reports to see where the money goes (channel performance, search terms and asset reporting), and test incrementality with a Performance Max uplift experiment or a geo holdout. Then compare what Google reports with closed revenue in your CRM. The in-platform conversion count alone tells you what Performance Max claimed, not what it caused.

This guide walks through each check, the exact reports to open, a worked example and the limits you should accept.

Why are Performance Max numbers hard to trust?

Performance Max (PMax) runs one campaign across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps, with Google choosing placements, audiences and bids. That automation creates three measurement risks:

  • It optimizes to whatever you count as a conversion. If a form fill, a click on WhatsApp or a page view counts, PMax will find cheap versions of it, including low-quality leads.
  • It can claim demand it did not create. Branded searches and returning visitors are easy conversions. Without exclusions, PMax may take credit for sales that would have come anyway.
  • It reports in Google's attribution, not yours. Google Ads counts conversions with its own model and windows, so its totals will rarely match your CRM. We explain why in why Meta, Google Ads and GA4 numbers don't match.

Step 1: Are you feeding Performance Max the right conversions?

Measurement starts before reporting. If PMax optimizes to raw leads, its reported results will look good while sales stay flat.

  1. List every conversion action set as primary in the campaign's goals. Remove page views, micro clicks and duplicate form events from bidding.
  2. Import offline outcomes from your CRM. Google's enhanced conversions for leads uses hashed first-party data, such as the email from the lead form, to match CRM outcomes like "qualified" or "won" back to ad clicks. See our guide to connecting offline conversions from your CRM.
  3. Send values. Give a qualified lead and a closed sale different values, or send the actual deal value, so Google can bid for revenue rather than volume.
  4. For ecommerce, report profit. Google's conversions with cart data combines the items in each order with the cost of goods sold from your Merchant Center feed to report gross profit. Item IDs must match your feed exactly. Our guide to POAS vs ROAS explains why profit is the better target.

Step 2: Which Google reports show where Performance Max spends?

Google has added more visibility to Performance Max over the last two years. Use it before you judge results.

Google's help article on the PMax search terms report recommends judging terms by conversions rather than clicks and impressions, because PMax also serves on YouTube and other channels, and notes that store visit and store sales conversions are not included. To block irrelevant searches, you can add negative keywords at the account or campaign level for Search and Shopping, and Google's Performance Max controls update describes shared negative keyword lists for PMax campaigns.

If a large share of conversion value comes from your own brand name, test brand exclusions. Google notes they apply to Search and Shopping inventory, with an option to let Shopping ads still appear for excluded brands.

Step 3: How do you test whether Performance Max is incremental?

Reports describe what happened. Only a test with a control group tells you whether PMax caused it. You have two practical options.

  1. Google's uplift experiment. In Experiments, Google offers uplift experiments for Performance Max that measure the incremental benefit of adding PMax to your existing Search, Video and Display campaigns. The default split is 50%, and Google picks "comparable campaigns" for the control automatically, which you can edit until the experiment ends.
  2. A geo holdout. Turn PMax off in a set of matched regions, for example a few states or cities, keep it on elsewhere, and compare CRM sales between the two groups over several weeks. This measures against your revenue, not Google's conversions. Our guide to holdout and geo tests covers the design.

Google publishes an average for context: advertisers that use Performance Max see "an average of 13% total incremental conversions" at a similar cost per action, according to Google's own Accelerate article. That is a vendor average across advertisers. Your account can be higher, lower or negative.

What are the limits of measuring Performance Max?

  • Experiments need volume. With a few dozen conversions a month, an uplift test may not reach a clear result. Run longer or use a bigger geo split.
  • Comparable campaigns can be messy. If the control includes campaigns that overlap with PMax, the measured uplift may be understated or overstated. Review the list Google picks.
  • Reporting is not keyword-level control. Search terms and channel reports show more than before, but you still cannot bid by keyword inside PMax.
  • Offline imports take time. If your sales cycle is 60 days, sales data for this month's leads arrives next quarter. Judge PMax on qualified leads first, sales later.
  • Features change. Google keeps adding controls and reports. Check your account for what is available today.

Who this is for, and when it doesn't apply

Good fit: lead-gen and B2B teams that sell after the click and need to know whether PMax leads become customers; ecommerce brands spending enough on PMax to run a test.

Doesn't apply as much: very small accounts with a handful of conversions a month. There, focus on clean conversion goals and brand exclusions, and keep test ambitions modest.

FAQ

Can I see which channels Performance Max spends on?

Yes. Google introduced a channel performance report for Performance Max that breaks results out by channel such as Search, YouTube and Display. Check Insights and reports in your account.

Does Performance Max take credit for brand searches?

It can. Check the PMax search terms report for brand terms and test brand exclusions, which apply to Search and Shopping inventory.

How do I know if Performance Max is incremental?

Run Google's Performance Max uplift experiment or a geo holdout and compare outcomes between treatment and control. Reported conversions alone cannot answer this.

How long should a Performance Max experiment run?

Google's help page does not set a fixed duration. Run it long enough to cover your conversion lag and collect enough conversions in each arm, usually several weeks.

Should lead-gen companies optimize PMax to form fills?

Only at the start. Move to qualified-lead or sale conversions imported from your CRM as soon as you have enough volume, so PMax stops buying cheap, low-quality leads.

Why don't PMax conversions match my CRM?

Google uses its own attribution model, windows and modeled conversions, and counts conversions your CRM may not record. Compare trends and cost per sale rather than expecting identical totals.
