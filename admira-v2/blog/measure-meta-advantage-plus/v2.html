How to Measure Meta Advantage+ Campaigns’ Real Impact

To measure the real impact of Meta Advantage+ campaigns, compare them against what would have happened without them: run a Meta Conversion Lift study or a geo holdout, check the share of results going to existing customers, and match Advantage+ leads and purchases to revenue in your own CRM or store data. Ads Manager ROAS alone is not enough, because Advantage+ automates audience, placement and budget, and much of what it reports can be sales you would have made anyway. Independent tests show the outcome varies by brand: in Haus's July 2025 analysis of Meta experiments, 42% of brands in head-to-head tests saw Advantage+ beat manual campaigns on incremental ROAS, and 58% saw manual do better.

This guide explains what Advantage+ is now, why its in-platform numbers need checking, the measurement methods from cheapest to most rigorous, and a worked example for a lead-gen account.

What counts as an Advantage+ campaign in 2026?

Meta has folded more automation into the default setup. As Jon Loomer documented in February 2025, Advantage+ Shopping campaigns were renamed Advantage+ Sales campaigns, and new Sales, App and Leads campaigns start with Advantage+ on by default. Three settings are on: Advantage+ audience, Advantage+ placements and Advantage+ campaign budget. Overriding some of them, such as restricting age or gender suggestions, can turn Advantage+ status off. The older Existing Customer Budget Cap has also been removed, according to later coverage, so you can no longer cap how much spend goes to past buyers inside the campaign itself.

In practice, the question is rarely "Advantage+ or not" for a single campaign. It's whether the automated setup produces more incremental sales per dollar than a more controlled one in your account.

Why can't you trust Advantage+ results in Ads Manager?

  • Existing customers. With broad targeting, delivery can reach people who already know you and would buy anyway. Ads Manager counts those purchases as conversions.
  • View-through credit. Default attribution includes conversions after an ad view without a click. See our post on 7-day click vs 1-day view.
  • Modeled conversions. Since iOS privacy changes, some conversions are estimated. Our guide to modeled conversions explains why.
  • Overlap with other channels. The same buyer may also click a Google ad, and both platforms will claim the sale.
  • Optimizing to the wrong event. For lead-gen, Advantage+ optimizes to whatever event you give it. If that's a form submit, it finds people who submit forms, not people who buy.

The Haus analysis also found that, on average across its tests, Advantage+ over-reported relative to measured incrementality by about 12 percentage points more than manual campaigns. Haus is a measurement vendor and its sample is its own customers, with iROAS measured on direct-to-consumer sales, so treat the figures as a signal that results vary, not as a rule.

How do you measure Advantage+ campaigns, step by step?

  1. Fix the optimization event. For lead-gen, send qualified leads and sales back through the Conversions API so Advantage+ optimizes to quality. Our guide on optimizing Meta ads for qualified leads covers the setup.
  2. Define existing customers and engaged audiences in Meta's audience segment settings with customer lists and custom audiences, then check the breakdown by audience segment. If most spend reaches existing customers, results will look better than they are.
  3. Tag every ad with {{campaign.id}} and {{ad.id}} in the URL parameters so leads carry the source into your CRM. See passing UTMs into your CRM.
  4. Match to revenue. Compare cost per qualified lead, cost per sale and revenue per dollar for Advantage+ vs your other campaigns, using CRM data, over at least one sales cycle.
  5. Run an A/B test of Advantage+ against a manual setup with the same budget, creative and event, if you're deciding between them.
  6. Run a lift or geo test before scaling Advantage+ to a large share of your budget. Our guide to holdout and geo tests covers design.
  7. Watch blended numbers. Total sales, MER and new-customer count should move when Advantage+ spend moves. If Meta reports a big win and total sales are flat, believe the total. See MER and blended ROAS.

Worked example: Advantage+ leads vs a manual campaign

Example (hypothetical numbers, not customer data): an education company tests an Advantage+ leads campaign against a manual campaign with interest targeting, $6,000 each for 30 days, same creative, both optimizing to form submits.

MetricAdvantage+Manual
Leads (Ads Manager)300200
Cost per lead$20$30
Qualified by admissions team (CRM)6070
Cost per qualified lead$100$86
Enrollments after 60 days912
Cost per enrollment$667$500

Ads Manager says Advantage+ wins by a third on cost per lead. The CRM says manual wins by a quarter on cost per enrollment. The next test is not to abandon Advantage+, but to rerun it optimizing to a qualified-lead event sent from the CRM, which gives the algorithm a better goal. Nine and twelve enrollments are small numbers, so repeat before drawing firm conclusions.

When this doesn't apply

If your account spends little or gets only a few conversions a week, lift studies and geo tests won't have enough data to read, and Meta's learning will be unstable either way. Start with the CRM match and the audience segment breakdown. If you sell only through an online Shopify checkout, store-level tools and Meta's own purchase reporting may cover most needs; ecommerce attribution tools are built for that case.

FAQ

Is Advantage+ better than manual campaigns?

It depends on the account. In Haus's 2025 analysis, 42% of brands saw Advantage+ win on incremental ROAS in head-to-head tests and 58% saw manual win. Test it in your own account.

What is a Conversion Lift study?

A Meta experiment that randomly holds back part of the audience from seeing ads, then compares conversions between the two groups to estimate incremental results. Eligibility varies by account.

Can I still limit Advantage+ spend on existing customers?

Not with the old in-campaign cap, which was removed. You can define existing customers to see the breakdown, or exclude them with a separate campaign structure.

Does Advantage+ work for lead generation?

It can, but it optimizes to the event you give it. Send qualified leads or sales from your CRM through the Conversions API so it doesn't optimize for cheap, low-intent form fills.

How long should an Advantage+ test run?

At least long enough to exit learning and cover one sales cycle. For lead-gen with 30 to 60 day cycles, judge on qualified leads first and on sales later.

Is Meta's incremental attribution the same as a lift test?

No. It's a modeled estimate built by Meta, while a lift test uses a randomized holdout. Use incremental attribution as a check and a lift or geo test for big decisions.
