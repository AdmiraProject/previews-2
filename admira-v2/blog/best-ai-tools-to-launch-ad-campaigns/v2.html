The best AI tools to launch ad campaigns start with the ones built into the ad platforms: Meta Advantage+, Google Performance Max and AI Max for Search, and TikTok Smart+ automate targeting, budget and placements at no extra software cost. On top of those, Madgicx (from $45/month, Meta only), Birch (from $49/month, Launcher on the $99 Pro plan) and Smartly (pricing not published, enterprise) help you launch and manage at scale. Pencil (from $14/month) and AdCreative.ai (from $39/month) focus on generating creative, and Pencil can also launch it. We checked prices on vendor pages in November 2026 and updated the list for 2027 planning. Admira publishes this guide and is included only for what its site states.

What does "launching campaigns with AI" actually mean?

It usually means one of three jobs, and most tools only do one or two:

  1. Automated delivery. The ad platform's AI chooses audiences, placements and budget split. This is what Advantage+, Performance Max and Smart+ do.
  2. Faster setup and launching. A third-party tool builds campaigns, ad sets and ads in bulk, or from a chat prompt, and pushes them to the ad account.
  3. Creative generation. AI writes copy and makes images or videos to feed those campaigns.

None of these tells you which campaigns produced sales. That needs measurement, which we cover at the end. For the creative side in depth, see our list of the best AI ad generators.

How we chose these tools

We kept tools that either launch campaigns with AI-driven automation inside Meta, Google or TikTok, or that create and push ads to those platforms. We read each vendor's pricing and help pages in November 2026 and only state features those pages describe. We did not run paid tests with each tool, so treat this as a structured shortlist.

Meta Advantage+: the default starting point on Meta

Advantage+ campaign creation covers the Sales, Leads and App Promotion objectives. Advantage+ campaign budget, audience and placements are on by default, and your targeting inputs act as suggestions. If you significantly restrict budget, audience or placements, Advantage+ turns off, as Jon Loomer's June 2025 guide documents.

Limits: you trade control for automation, and Meta's own reporting grades its own work. Read how to measure Advantage+ real impact.

Google Performance Max and AI Max: the default on Google

Google describes Performance Max as a goal-based campaign that reaches all Google Ads inventory from one campaign, using Smart Bidding and moving budget to the best-performing placements. You provide conversion goals, assets and audience signals. AI Max for Search adds broad-match and keywordless query matching, text customization and final URL expansion to Search campaigns, and Google recommends conversion-based bidding with it.

Limits: if your conversion data is weak (for example, every form fill counts as a win), the AI optimizes toward the wrong goal. See how to measure Performance Max results.

TikTok Smart+: automation on TikTok

TikTok's help center describes Smart+ as AI-powered campaigns that automate targeting, optimization and creative. The upgraded flow lets advertisers choose full, partial or manual control, and Smart+ supports lead generation with website, Instant Form and messaging destinations.

Limits: some newer features may need early access through an account manager.

Madgicx: an AI media buyer for Meta

Madgicx says plans start at $45/month, priced by monthly ad spend, with a 7-day free trial. Its AI Marketer audits Meta accounts daily and prepares actions; Madgicx says you can choose autonomous execution or a human-approved workflow.

Limits: Madgicx states it optimizes Meta ads only. Google and TikTok data appear in reporting, not launching.

Birch: rules and bulk launching

Birch (formerly Revealbot) lists Essential at $49/month and Pro at $99/month, with Launcher, automated rules and an audience builder on Pro. It supports Meta, Google, Snapchat and TikTok, prices by monthly ad spend and offers a 14-day free trial without a card.

Limits: its pricing page does not mention AI features, so think of it as automation and bulk launching, not AI campaign creation.

Smartly: enterprise launching across channels

Smartly describes itself as an AI advertising platform with Creative, Media and Intelligence suites, covering automated campaign setup across social, connected TV, open web and video. It targets agencies, performance, brand and B2B teams.

Limits: pricing is not published; you go through a demo. It is usually more than a small team needs.

Pencil: creative agents that can also launch

Pencil lists Core at $14/month ($11 annual, 50 generations) and Growth at $55/month ($44 annual, 250 generations), with Pro on custom pricing. Its pricing page says Core and up can "launch & track ads" on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Google Display, DV360 and LinkedIn, and includes self-serve AI agents for text, images and video.

Limits: Google Search is not listed, and the page does not say how much campaign setup it covers inside each platform.

AdCreative.ai: bulk creative, not a launcher

AdCreative.ai's homepage lists Starter at $39/month on monthly billing, Professional at $249/month and an Enterprise tier on request; it showed conflicting Ultimate prices. Every plan lists ad platform integrations.

Limits: its site does not confirm that it launches campaigns directly, so plan to launch elsewhere.

Admira: AI ads tied to revenue

Admira's AI ad creative generator pages say you can "plan, create and launch video and static ads from one place" and then see which creative drove revenue. The site lists Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn and Click-to-WhatsApp ads as supported. AI Ads, AI CMO and "unlimited ad creations, custom assets & edits" are included in the Growth plan at $399/month. Its Conversion API sends CRM sales back to Meta, Google and TikTok, so the platforms' own AI can optimize toward real sales.

Limits: the site does not explain how launches are pushed, or how budget and targeting are set, so confirm the workflow in a demo. Admira is a young brand with few public reviews.

How do you launch AI campaigns safely?

  1. Fix your conversion signal first. Send qualified leads or sales, not every form fill, through the Conversions API.
  2. Set guardrails: budget caps, brand exclusions, locations and any required audience exclusions.
  3. Use an approval step when a tool offers one, at least for the first month.
  4. Launch with a few clear concepts instead of dozens of tiny variations.
  5. Judge by revenue per creative after a full sales cycle. Our guide to creative-level attribution shows how.

When AI launching doesn't apply

AI delivery needs volume. If you get only a handful of conversions a month, the algorithm has little to learn from, and a simple manual campaign with tight targeting can be easier to read. Regulated categories (credit, housing, health) also face targeting limits that reduce what automation can do.

FAQ

Can AI launch a Meta campaign by itself?

Meta's Advantage+ automates budget, audience and placements once you set up the campaign. Some tools, such as Madgicx, describe autonomous or approval-based actions on Meta accounts.

Are Advantage+ and Performance Max free?

There is no separate software fee; you pay for the media you buy.

Which tool launches on Meta, Google and TikTok?

Birch lists Meta, Google, Snapchat and TikTok. Pencil lists Meta placements, TikTok, YouTube, Google Display, DV360 and LinkedIn. Smartly covers many channels on enterprise plans.

Do I still need a media buyer?

Yes, for strategy, offers, creative direction and guardrails. AI tools reduce setup and optimization work, not the need for judgment.

How do I know if AI campaigns drive sales?

Compare CRM revenue by campaign and creative, not only platform-reported conversions, and run holdout tests where you can.

Does Admira launch ads?

Its site says you can plan, create and launch video and static ads from one place and lists Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn and Click-to-WhatsApp. Ask for a demo to see exactly how launching works.
