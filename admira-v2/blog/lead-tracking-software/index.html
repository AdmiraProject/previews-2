The best lead tracking software in 2026 depends on where your leads arrive: CallRail and WhatConverts are strongest for phone calls and forms at low cost, Leadsources is a budget option for form source capture, Ruler Analytics and Admira connect leads to closed revenue in the CRM, HubSpot works if you already live in it, and Kommo suits teams that sell through WhatsApp chats. Published starting prices range from about $30 to $400 per month (checked November 2026), and most tools charge by usage, visits or leads.

Below is a side-by-side table, a short review of each tool, a cost example and a checklist to pick one.

What should lead tracking software do?

At minimum, a lead tracking tool should record the source of every lead (channel, campaign, ad, keyword), cover every contact method you use (forms, calls, chat, WhatsApp), save that data in your CRM, and show which sources produce sales, not just leads. Better tools also send qualified leads and sales back to ad platforms so campaigns optimize for buyers. If the concept is new, start with what lead tracking is.

Which tool fits which situation?

Admira: for multi-channel ads that close in a CRM or on WhatsApp

Admira ties leads from Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and Click-to-WhatsApp to revenue in HubSpot, Salesforce, Kommo, Clientify or ActiveCampaign, and sends CRM stages back to the ad platforms through a server-side Conversion API. Tracking is cookieless by default, and support is in Spanish and English. Plans: Starter $199/month, Growth $399/month (30,000 visitors, 6 seats), Custom $1,500/month. Limits: it is a young brand with few public reviews, call tracking numbers are not among its listed features, and pure Shopify stores may be better served by an ecommerce tool. More on the lead tracking page.

CallRail: for businesses where the phone rings

CallRail's Lead Tracking plan includes call tracking with 5 local numbers and 250 local minutes; Lead Tracking Complete ($95/month) adds form tracking with 1,000 submissions. Usage above the included amounts costs extra. It is a strong pick for home services, clinics and law firms. See our guide to phone call attribution.

WhatConverts: for the lowest entry price

WhatConverts starts at $30/month for call tracking, $60 with forms and chat, and $160 for Elite, which adds customer journey and multi-click attribution. Agency plans with unlimited accounts run from $500 to $1,250/month.

Leadsources: for form source data only

Leadsources captures channel, source, UTMs, landing page, device and a multi-touch journey on each form lead. Plans run from $48/month (250 leads) to $280/month for agencies, with a 14-day trial. It does not cover calls or WhatsApp chats.

Ruler Analytics: for revenue attribution with white-glove onboarding

Ruler lists indicative prices from £299 / $400 per month up to £1,499 / $2,000 on Advanced, which adds marketing mix modeling. Pricing scales with traffic and integrations. Compare it with peers in Ruler Analytics alternatives.

HubSpot: for teams already on HubSpot

HubSpot records original source on every contact and has attribution reports, but revenue attribution needs Marketing Hub Enterprise ($3,600/month on annual billing plus a $7,000 onboarding fee, checked Nov 2026). It does not provide dynamic call tracking numbers on its own. Read the limits of HubSpot attribution.

Kommo: for WhatsApp-first sales pipelines

Kommo is a messaging CRM rather than a tracking tool. It captures leads from chats and lead form ads from Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn, and its Pro plan lists campaign ROI tracking. Billing starts at a 6-month minimum with no monthly option. For ad-level revenue, teams often pair it with an attribution layer; see measuring sales from ads in Kommo.

How do you choose? A 6-step checklist

  1. List your contact methods. Forms, calls, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, walk-ins. Rule out tools that miss a channel that brings real sales.
  2. Check your CRM integration. Native sync to HubSpot, Salesforce, Kommo or Clientify beats CSV exports.
  3. Ask how source is stored. First touch and last touch, on the contact and the deal.
  4. Confirm the pricing meter. Visits, leads, minutes or users. Model your volume at peak season.
  5. Check the feedback loop. Can it send qualified leads and sales back to Meta and Google?
  6. Run a two-week pilot. Compare its lead counts with your CRM before you sign an annual plan.

Who this comparison is for, and when it doesn't apply

For: lead-gen businesses, agencies and B2B teams choosing a tool to know where leads come from and which ones buy.

Not for: ecommerce stores with online checkout, where order-level tools fit better, or companies that need a full marketing mix model rather than lead-level tracking.

FAQ

What is the cheapest lead tracking software?

Among the tools here, WhatConverts starts at $30/month for call tracking and Leadsources at $48/month for form source capture (checked Nov 2026).

Can a CRM replace lead tracking software?

Partly. A CRM with hidden UTM fields can store source data, but it usually cannot track calls, WhatsApp referrals or send outcomes back to ad platforms without extra tools.

Which tool tracks WhatsApp leads?

Kommo captures chats as leads, and Admira connects Click-to-WhatsApp ads to CRM revenue. Call and form trackers usually do not cover WhatsApp.

Do I need call tracking?

If more than a small share of your sales start with a phone call, yes. Without tracking numbers, those calls show up as direct or unknown.

How long does setup take?

Script and form tracking usually takes under a day. CRM sync, call numbers and offline conversions can take one to two weeks.

Is Admira a call tracking tool?

Call tracking is not among Admira's listed features. It tracks leads from ads, forms and Click-to-WhatsApp and ties them to CRM revenue, so pair it with a call tracker if calls are a major channel.
