The best Ruler Analytics alternatives in 2026 are CallRail and WhatConverts if you mainly need call and form tracking for less money, Dreamdata or HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise if you are a B2B team that wants account-level revenue attribution, Cometly if you run paid social into HubSpot or Salesforce, and Admira if your leads close in a CRM or on WhatsApp in Mexico or LATAM. Ruler itself starts at $400/month (£299) for up to 10,000 monthly visits on monthly billing, so most alternatives win on either price or a specific channel.

Every price below was checked on the vendor's own pricing page in November 2026. Where a vendor does not publish a price, we say so instead of guessing.

What does Ruler Analytics do, and why look for an alternative?

Ruler is a marketing attribution platform that ties calls, forms and chats to CRM revenue. Its pricing page lists dynamic call tracking, form tracking (including Calendly, Typeform and Gravity Forms), and CRM integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics 365, Pipedrive, Zoho, Monday and others. Every plan includes a dedicated customer success manager and onboarding.

Ruler calls these prices indicative and scales them by traffic, product and integration needs. Data-driven and impression attribution start on Medium; marketing mix modeling and its AI agent are on Advanced.

Teams usually look elsewhere for one of four reasons:

  • Budget. $400/month is a lot if you only need call tracking.
  • Channel fit. WhatsApp is not among the lead sources on Ruler's pricing page, which matters in LATAM.
  • B2B depth. Account-based teams may want company-level journeys.
  • Feature tiers. Data-driven attribution and MMM sit on higher plans.

Which alternative fits which team?

1. CallRail: best for call-heavy local businesses

CallRail's Lead Tracking plan ($50/month) includes call and text tracking, recording and transcription, with 5 local numbers and 250 local minutes. Lead Tracking Complete ($95/month) adds form tracking and multi-touch cost-per-lead reporting. Extra local numbers cost $3 each. It is a strong fit if phone calls are your main conversion. It is less of a fit if you need closed revenue from a CRM pipeline, which is Ruler's core strength.

2. WhatConverts: best low-cost lead tracking

WhatConverts starts at $30/month for call tracking, $60 with forms and chat, and $160 for Elite, which adds customer journey and multi-click attribution. Agency plans with unlimited accounts run $500 to $1,250/month. Choose it if you want lead-level source data on a small budget and can live with simpler revenue reporting.

3. Dreamdata: best for B2B account journeys

Dreamdata offers a free plan with B2B web analytics, company identification and an ad spend report (2 months of history, 5 seats). Attribution models and revenue analytics are on its Advanced plan, which is priced on request. It fits B2B SaaS teams with long, multi-stakeholder cycles. Read our guide to B2B SaaS attribution for long sales cycles.

4. HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise: best if you already live in HubSpot

HubSpot's deal-create and revenue attribution reports require Marketing Hub Enterprise, per its knowledge base. Professional only gets contact-create attribution. It is convenient but expensive, and it only sees what HubSpot tracks. We cover the gaps in is HubSpot attribution reporting enough.

5. Cometly: best for paid social into a CRM pipeline

Cometly connects Meta, Google, LinkedIn, TikTok, Microsoft, Reddit and Snapchat, and maps clicks to HubSpot and Salesforce deals. It prices by pageviews, does not publish amounts and has no free trial. Compare it in Hyros vs Cometly.

6. Admira: best for WhatsApp and LATAM lead-gen

Admira ties Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and Click-to-WhatsApp leads to revenue in HubSpot, Salesforce, Kommo or Clientify, and sends CRM events back to ad platforms with its Conversion API. Plans are Starter $199/month, Growth $399/month (30,000 visitors, 6 seats) and Custom $1,500/month. The honest limits: it is a young brand with few public reviews, and it does not advertise call tracking, so a phone-first business should pair it with a call tool or pick CallRail. How do you choose a Ruler alternative? (5 steps)

  1. Rank your lead sources. Count last quarter's leads by type: calls, forms, chat, WhatsApp. The biggest bucket decides your shortlist.
  2. Find your revenue event. Is it a closed-won deal in a CRM, or a qualified call? Tools that stop at the lead cannot report ROAS on closed revenue.
  3. Check the CRM connector. Confirm a native integration with your CRM, including stage changes and deal value.
  4. Price at your volume. Use your real visits, numbers and minutes. Usage fees change the math on call tracking tools.
  5. Run a 30-day parallel test. Keep Ruler running, compare matched revenue, then switch.

When should you stay with Ruler?

Stay if you need dynamic call tracking, form tracking and CRM revenue attribution in one tool with a dedicated CSM, especially in the UK where Ruler prices in pounds. Stay if you plan to use its marketing mix modeling on the Advanced plan. Switching only pays off when an alternative fixes a real gap: price, WhatsApp, B2B account depth or ad-platform feedback.

FAQ

How much does Ruler Analytics cost?

Ruler lists Small at £299 / $400 per month for up to 10,000 visits, up to Advanced at £1,499 / $2,000 from 100,000 visits. Annual billing saves 10%. Ruler calls these prices indicative.

What is the cheapest Ruler alternative?

WhatConverts starts at $30/month and CallRail at $50/month. Both focus on lead tracking and offer 14-day trials, but neither replaces full CRM revenue attribution on its entry plan.

Does Ruler Analytics have a free trial?

Its pricing page does not mention one; it points to "Get Started" or a demo request.

Which Ruler alternative works with WhatsApp?

Admira supports Click-to-WhatsApp ads, Kommo, Clientify and the WhatsApp Business Platform. The other tools here do not list WhatsApp on their pricing pages.

Is Dreamdata a good Ruler alternative?

For B2B teams that want account-level journeys, yes. Its free plan covers web analytics and company identification, while attribution sits on the Advanced plan with custom pricing.

Can HubSpot replace Ruler?

Only partly. Revenue attribution requires Marketing Hub Enterprise ($3,600/month annual), and HubSpot does not do dynamic call tracking on its own.
