Hyros fits info-product, coaching and call-based businesses that want revenue-priced tracking with a setup team, while Cometly fits SaaS, ecommerce and B2B teams that want pageview-priced attribution with native HubSpot and Salesforce syncs. Both track first-party, send conversions back to Meta and Google server-side, and offer multi-touch reporting. The real decision comes down to three things: how each tool charges you as you grow, which CRM and sales motion you run, and how much setup help you need.

We checked both vendors' public pricing and product pages in November 2026. Where a number comes from the vendor, we say so. Where it is not published, we say that too. We also cover when neither tool is the right fit.

How does Hyros pricing compare with Cometly pricing?

They charge on different meters, so the cheaper tool depends on your ratio of revenue to traffic.

  • Hyros charges on revenue it tracks. Your bill rises when your sales rise, even if traffic stays flat. A high-ticket offer with modest traffic pays more per visitor.
  • Cometly charges on pageviews. Your bill rises when traffic rises, even if revenue stays flat. A content-heavy site with low order values pays more per dollar of revenue. Cometly says it does not throttle attribution when you pass your limit; you get a notice at 90% and move up a tier at the next billing period.

Cometly does not publish a number, so you need a quote at ~22,500 pageviews. Ask both vendors for a price at today's volume and at 2x, because the meter that looks cheap now may not be cheap after a good year. Which one tracks sales more accurately?

There is no independent public benchmark that compares Hyros and Cometly accuracy on the same data, so be skeptical of any article that declares a winner. Both vendors publish their own performance claims; those are marketing, not audits.

In practice, accuracy depends less on the logo and more on four setup details:

  1. Identity capture. The tool must connect an anonymous click to an email or phone at opt-in. If your forms or checkout skip this, any tool loses the thread.
  2. The revenue event. Stripe payments, Shopify orders or CRM closed-won deals must flow in with the right value and date.
  3. Attribution window. Hyros states windows up to 1 year, which matters for long sales cycles. Ask Cometly what window it applies to your account.
  4. Deduplication. When conversions go back to Meta and Google server-side, the browser pixel and the server event must share an event ID or you double count. Our guide on what server-side tracking fixes explains why.

The fair test: run both demos on one real campaign, pull the last 30 days of closed revenue from your CRM or payment processor, and see which tool matches more of it to an ad.

Which one works better with your CRM and sales process?

This is usually the deciding factor.

  • Funnel and call-based offers: Hyros lists GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels, Kajabi and Stripe, plus call tracking that reports bookings, call quality and close rate by rep. If your sales team closes on the phone after a webinar, that is a strong fit.
  • B2B and SaaS with a formal pipeline: Cometly says it maps ad clicks to HubSpot contacts, deals and lifecycle changes, and pipes Salesforce leads, opportunities and closed-won deals into attribution. Enterprise adds warehouse exports. If your source of truth is a CRM with stages, Cometly is closer to that model.
  • WhatsApp-first sales: in our research, neither vendor lists a native Kommo or Clientify connector. Click-to-WhatsApp attribution needs the click ID (ctwa_clid) that Meta attaches to the first message, and events sent through Meta's Conversions API for Business Messaging. Ask both vendors to show this live before you sign.

How do you choose between Hyros and Cometly? (6 steps)

  1. Define the money event. Is it a Stripe payment, a Shopify order, a showed-up sales call, or a closed-won deal?
  2. Find where it lives. List the system that records it: Stripe, Shopify, GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce or a WhatsApp CRM.
  3. Pull your meters. Get last quarter's monthly tracked revenue and sessions. Request quotes at 1x and 2x.
  4. Demo on your data. Pick one campaign and compare tool-reported revenue against your CRM or processor.
  5. Check what goes back to ads. Confirm which events, values and IDs each tool sends to Meta and Google, and how it dedupes.
  6. Read the exit terms. Contract length, refund conditions and whether you can export raw touchpoint data if you leave.

FAQ

Is Hyros more expensive than Cometly?

It depends on your revenue-to-traffic ratio. Hyros prices by tracked revenue (from $230/month on annual billing up to $20k), while Cometly prices by pageviews and does not publish amounts. Get quotes at your real volume.

Does Hyros or Cometly offer a free trial?

Cometly says it has no free trial and offers paid onboarding instead. Hyros does not list a trial on its pricing page but states a 90-day refund if results are not achieved.

Which is better for B2B with long sales cycles?

Cometly's HubSpot and Salesforce syncs map closer to a B2B pipeline. Hyros's attribution windows of up to 1 year also help long cycles, so test both against your closed-won data.

Do both send conversions back to Meta and Google?

Yes. Hyros lists Meta Conversions API, Google Offline Conversions and TikTok Events API. Cometly lists server-side tracking and Conversion API on its Core plan.

Can either track WhatsApp sales?

We did not find a native WhatsApp CRM connector (Kommo, Clientify) for either. Ask for a live demo of Click-to-WhatsApp attribution using Meta's click ID before buying.

Which has more integrations?

Cometly says it has 70+ integrations and connects seven ad platforms. Hyros lists four ad platforms plus Shopify, WooCommerce, funnel builders and 30+ payment processors.
