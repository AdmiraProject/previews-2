The best Northbeam alternatives in 2026 are Triple Whale for Shopify brands that want a free entry point, Rockerbox for teams that want MTA, MMM and incrementality testing from one vendor, Polar Analytics for a warehouse-backed ecommerce BI stack, Wicked Reports for cohort and LTV reporting from $499/month, Hyros or Cometly for paid-media tracking, and Admira for brands and lead-gen teams whose sales close in a CRM or WhatsApp. Which one fits depends mainly on your ad budget, your sales channel and whether you need modeling beyond multi-touch attribution.

For context, Northbeam's own pricing page (checked November 2026) lists Starter at $1,500 for brands spending under $1.5M a year on ads, Professional at $3,500/month for brands spending up to $500K a month, and a custom Enterprise plan with optional MMM+ and incrementality. Professional and Enterprise are annual terms, and the monthly price is "informed by the volume of data in your business (measured in pageviews)" (Northbeam pricing).

Why do brands look for Northbeam alternatives?

  • Entry cost. A starting point of $1,500 and $3,500/month for Professional is a big share of budget for brands spending under $50,000/month on ads.
  • Annual terms. Professional and Enterprise are billed on annual or semi-annual agreements.
  • Different measurement needs. Some teams want MMM or incrementality as the core method rather than as an add-on.
  • Non-ecommerce sales. Northbeam is built around ecommerce platforms. Brands or lead-gen teams that close sales via WhatsApp, phone or a CRM need a different data source.

Which Northbeam alternative fits your situation?

Triple Whale: the Shopify-native option

Triple Whale has a free plan with first and last click attribution and 12 months of data retention, then paid Foundation, Automate and Enterprise plans priced on GMV with unlimited users (per a third-party pricing review checked November 4, 2026). It's the most common first comparison. Read Triple Whale vs Northbeam vs Admira and our Triple Whale alternatives.

Rockerbox: for MTA plus MMM and testing

Rockerbox's site lists multi-touch attribution, marketing mix modeling and incrementality testing, with de-duplicated, user-level attribution and 100+ integrations. It says testing can start in one to two weeks, while MTA or MMM can take six to eight weeks (Rockerbox). Limits: pricing isn't published, and third-party estimates vary widely, so get a quote.

Polar Analytics: for an ecommerce data warehouse

Polar includes a dedicated Snowflake database, a first-party pixel with attribution, unlimited users and history, and per-test incrementality on its Custom plan (Polar pricing). Good if your team wants SQL access and a single ecommerce data layer.

Wicked Reports: for LTV-heavy brands

Wicked Reports publishes four tiers from $499 to $4,999+/month based on annual revenue, with cohort and LTV reports and lifetime lookback (Wicked Reports pricing). It suits subscription brands where first-order ROAS hides the real return.

Hyros and Cometly: for paid-media tracking

Both track ads and send conversions back to the platforms. Neither publishes prices. See Cometly alternatives for a closer look.

Admira: when revenue lives in a CRM

If your brand sells high-ticket products through Click-to-WhatsApp ads, sales reps or showrooms, the sale shows up in Kommo, HubSpot or Salesforce, not in a checkout. Admira's multi-touch attribution ties Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and WhatsApp leads to that CRM revenue and sends qualified events back through the Conversions API. Pricing is public from $199/month, and setup needs no engineering. Limits: Admira is not an MMM consultancy, it's a young brand with few public reviews, and for a pure Shopify store with online checkout an ecommerce-first tool may fit better.

How do you choose and switch safely?

  1. Define the decision. Daily campaign optimization, quarterly budget splits or channel validation each point to a different tool.
  2. Map your revenue source. Shopify, another ecommerce platform, a CRM or WhatsApp.
  3. Collect pricing inputs. Monthly ad spend, pageviews, annual GMV and orders, so every quote uses the same numbers.
  4. Ask about terms. Monthly or annual, onboarding included or not, and what happens when you cross a tier.
  5. Run in parallel for 30 days. Compare both tools against actual revenue and blended MER, not against each other.
  6. Time the switch. Move at the start of a reporting period so trend lines stay comparable.

When should you stay with Northbeam?

Stay if you're a DTC brand with enough spend to justify it, your team uses the media strategist support, and you plan to add incrementality or MMM+ through the same vendor. Northbeam's plans also include up to $50,000 to $200,000 in ad credits from platform partners depending on tier, which can offset part of the cost.

FAQ

What is the best alternative to Northbeam?

Triple Whale for Shopify brands, Rockerbox for MTA plus MMM and testing, Polar for a warehouse-backed stack, Wicked Reports for LTV, and Admira when sales close in a CRM or WhatsApp.

How much does Northbeam cost in 2026?

Northbeam's page lists Starter at $1,500, Professional at $3,500/month for brands spending up to $500K a month on ads, and custom Enterprise pricing, with final prices informed by data volume.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Northbeam?

Yes. Wicked Reports starts at $499/month, Admira at $199/month, and Triple Whale has a free plan. Check that the cheaper tool covers your channels and revenue source.

Does Northbeam offer marketing mix modeling?

Yes, MMM+ is an optional add-on on the Enterprise plan, and incrementality is optional on Professional and Enterprise.

Which Northbeam alternative includes MMM?

Rockerbox lists MMM alongside MTA and incrementality, and Ruler Analytics includes MMM on its Advanced plan. Admira is not an MMM tool.

Can a Northbeam alternative track WhatsApp sales?

Admira attributes Click-to-WhatsApp leads and matches them to revenue in CRMs like Kommo and Clientify. Most ecommerce tools only read checkout orders.
