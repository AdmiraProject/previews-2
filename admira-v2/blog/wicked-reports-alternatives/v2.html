The main Wicked Reports alternatives in 2026 are Triple Whale and Lifetimely for Shopify brands, Northbeam for larger DTC budgets, Hyros and Cometly for paid-media tracking with call and funnel sales, SegMetrics for course and funnel businesses on a budget, Ruler Analytics for UK and EU lead-gen, and Admira for lead-gen and WhatsApp sales that close in a CRM. Wicked Reports itself starts at $499/month for brands up to $2.5M in annual revenue (checked November 2026), so most people switch for a lower entry price, a better fit with their sales motion, or both.

Below: what Wicked Reports does well, a comparison table with verified prices, a short take on each option and a cost example.

What is Wicked Reports, and why look for alternatives?

Wicked Reports describes itself as marketing attribution and optimization for ecommerce brands and agencies, with a dedicated focus on subscription brands that need to tie repeat rebills back to the first customer touch. Its strengths are new-customer versus repeat reporting, cohort and LTV reports with lifetime lookback, an "attribution time machine" to re-run periods under different models, and Advanced Signal, which sends Meta a verified new-customer purchase event (Wicked Reports).

Which alternative fits which business?

Admira: when sales close in a CRM or on WhatsApp

Admira ties Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and Click-to-WhatsApp leads to revenue in HubSpot, Salesforce, Kommo or Clientify, with attribution models, buyer journeys and a server-side Conversion API that sends CRM stages back to the ad platforms. Plans: Starter $199/month (10,000 visitors), Growth $399/month (30,000 visitors, 6 seats), Custom $1,500/month. Limits: a young brand with few public reviews, and for a pure Shopify store with subscriptions, Wicked Reports or an ecommerce tool may fit better. Admira is not an MMM consultancy.

Hyros: for funnels and high-ticket sales

Hyros prices by tracked revenue, from $230/month on annual billing up to $1,499/month for up to $750k, with custom pricing above $1M, and says all plans include full features. It targets info products, coaching and media buyers spending $10,000+ a month. Compare it in Hyros alternatives.

Cometly: for paid-media teams

Cometly offers pixel and server-side tracking, multi-touch attribution, a Conversion API and an AI Ads Manager on its Core plan, priced by pageviews with no published amounts and no free trial. See Cometly alternatives.

Triple Whale and Lifetimely: for Shopify

Triple Whale has a free plan and paid tiers based on store revenue. Lifetimely, part of AMP, is free up to 50 orders per month and from $149/month for 3,000 orders, focused on profit and LTV. Both are closer to Wicked Reports' ecommerce use case. Our Triple Whale alternatives post goes deeper.

Northbeam: for bigger DTC budgets

Northbeam's page shows Starter at $1,500 for brands under $1.5M a year in ad spend and Professional at $3,500/month, with custom Enterprise. It is a step up in price from Wicked Reports, not a saving.

SegMetrics and Ruler: for funnels and lead-gen

SegMetrics starts at $57/month for up to 10K contacts, with Grow at $197 and Scale at $397. Ruler starts at $400/month and scales with traffic, with marketing mix modeling on Advanced ($2,000/month).

What would switching cost? A worked example

Example (hypothetical numbers): a supplement brand does $1.8M a year online and spends $25,000/month on Meta and Google. A second business, a financial education company, makes $150,000 a month in tracked revenue through webinars and sales calls, with 22,000 visitors a month.

The lesson: for subscription ecommerce, Wicked Reports' cohort and new-customer reporting is hard to replace for less. For lead-based sales, the cheaper option is often also the better fit, because it tracks pipeline stages instead of orders.

How do you choose? A 5-step checklist

  1. Name your conversion. Online order, subscription rebill, booked call or CRM deal. Pick tools built for that unit.
  2. Model the pricing meter. Revenue, visitors, pageviews or orders. Run your peak month through each.
  3. Check integrations. Your cart (Shopify, WooCommerce) or CRM (HubSpot, Kommo, Clientify), plus every ad platform you use.
  4. Confirm the feedback loop. Does it send conversions back to Meta and Google server-side?
  5. Pilot before you migrate. Run both tools for two to four weeks and reconcile against your store or CRM. Use our 15 questions for attribution vendors.

Who this is for, and when you should stay on Wicked Reports

For: teams paying for Wicked Reports that sell through leads, calls or WhatsApp, smaller brands for whom $499 is a large share of spend, and buyers comparing it before signing.

Stay if: you run a subscription or repeat-purchase ecommerce brand that relies on cohort LTV, new-customer CAC and lifetime lookback. Those are Wicked Reports' core strengths.

FAQ

How much does Wicked Reports cost in 2026?

Measure is $499/month for brands up to $2.5M in annual revenue, Scale $699, Maximize $999, and Enterprise from $4,999 (checked Nov 2026).

What is the cheapest Wicked Reports alternative?

Lifetimely has a free plan up to 50 orders a month, SegMetrics starts at $57/month and Admira at $199/month. Each fits a different type of business.

Is Wicked Reports good for lead generation?

Its site is aimed at ecommerce brands and agencies and does not focus on lead-gen. Lead-based businesses usually need CRM pipeline stages, which tools like Admira, Ruler or Hyros handle more directly.

Does Wicked Reports send data to Meta's Conversions API?

Yes, through Advanced Signal, which is included on Maximize and Enterprise or costs $199/month on Measure and Scale.

Which alternative is best for Shopify subscription brands?

Triple Whale and Lifetimely are the closest fits, though for cohort-based subscription reporting Wicked Reports remains a strong option.

Can I migrate historical data from Wicked Reports?

Most tools rebuild history from your ad platforms, store or CRM rather than importing another vendor's attribution. Ask each vendor how far back they can backfill.
