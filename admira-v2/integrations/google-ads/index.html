What is the Google Ads integration?

Google Ads gives Admira the spend, the campaigns and every click. Your CRM gives it the leads, deals and sales. Admira joins the two, so each Google Ads campaign, ad set and ad is judged by the revenue it produced, not by the conversions the platform reports.

Admira AI answers from the same data. Ask which Google Ads campaigns brought revenue this quarter and get the answer with its sources, in seconds.
