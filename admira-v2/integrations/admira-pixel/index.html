What is the Admira pixel integration?

The Admira pixel is the script on your site. It records every visit with its UTMs and click IDs, and when a visitor shares an email or phone, Admira links the whole history to that contact.

Admira AI answers from the same data. Ask which channels brought revenue this quarter and get the answer with its sources, in seconds.
