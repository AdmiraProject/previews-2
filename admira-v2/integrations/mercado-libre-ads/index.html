What is the Mercado Libre Ads integration?

Mercado Libre Ads gives Admira the spend, the campaigns and every click. Your CRM gives it the leads, deals and sales. Admira joins the two, so each Mercado Libre Ads campaign, ad set and ad is judged by the revenue it produced, not by the conversions the platform reports.

Admira AI answers from the same data. Ask which Mercado Libre Ads campaigns brought revenue this quarter and get the answer with its sources, in seconds.
