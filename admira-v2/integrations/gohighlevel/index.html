What is the GoHighLevel integration?

GoHighLevel holds your leads, deals and revenue. Admira matches each one to the ad clicks, visits and chats that came before, so you see which campaigns bring customers, not just traffic.

Admira AI answers from the same data. Ask which channels brought revenue this quarter and get the answer with its sources, in seconds.
