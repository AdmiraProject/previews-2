We NEVER sell your data to anyone.

Admira considers data protection and privacy to be of paramount importance. We never sell Personal Data and we carry out all processing operations in compliance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) (specifically but not limited to Article 6(1)(b) to (f) and Article 28) and other applicable global privacy and data protection laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act (“CCPA”) (collectively, the “Applicable Law”).

For this Privacy Policy, "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (or household as applicable); an identifiable natural person is one who can be identified, directly or indirectly, in particular by reference to an identifier such as a name, an identification number, location data, an online identifier or to one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural or social identity of that natural person. Personal Data also includes any equivalent definition in the Applicable Law.

How We Protect Your Data

Admira applies the following technical and organizational measures to all customer data, including data we retrieve from connected advertising and CRM platforms on your behalf.

Encryption in transit. All traffic between you, our applications and our APIs is encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher. Plain HTTP requests are redirected to HTTPS, and older TLS versions are rejected. Connections between our services and our databases require TLS as well.

Encryption at rest. All databases and all object storage are encrypted at rest using AES-256 with keys managed by AWS Key Management Service (KMS). Automated database backups are encrypted with the same keys and are retained for a limited period before being deleted automatically.

Additional protection for access credentials. The OAuth tokens and API credentials you authorize are protected with envelope encryption on top of the storage-level encryption described above: each credential is sealed with AES-256-GCM under its own single-use data encryption key, which is itself wrapped by AWS KMS and cryptographically bound to your workspace, so key material issued for one customer cannot decrypt another customer’s credentials. Credentials are never returned through our API and are stripped from application logs and error reports.

Access control. Our databases are not reachable from the public internet; they accept connections only from within our private network. Access to production systems is granted to a limited number of authorized Admira personnel through individually named accounts under least-privilege permissions, and only where it is necessary to operate the Service or to resolve a support issue you have raised.

Hosting. All customer data is hosted on Amazon Web Services in the US East (Ohio) region in the United States, including the data pipeline that retrieves data from connected platforms, which runs entirely inside our own infrastructure.

Deletion. When you disconnect a data source, we delete the stored credential for that source, drop the tables holding the data retrieved from it, and delete the associated records. When you delete your Admira account, personal data we process on your behalf is permanently deleted within 30 calendar days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

Google User Data and Limited Use

When you connect a Google Ads account to Admira, Google asks you to grant specific permissions. We request only the scopes required by the features you connect:

Google Ads (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords) - to read the performance data of the Google Ads accounts you select (campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords, costs, clicks, impressions and conversions), so that Admira can show you reporting and attribution across your marketing channels.

Data Manager (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/datamanager) - used only if you enable conversion upload, to send back to your own Google Ads account the conversion events you have configured in Admira.

Data retrieved from Google APIs is stored and protected exactly as described in "How We Protect Your Data" above, and is used solely to provide the Admira features you have enabled for your workspace.

Limited Use. Admira’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy (https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

We do not sell Google user data, and we do not use it for advertising purposes of our own.

We do not use Google user data to develop, train, improve or fine-tune generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

We do not transfer Google user data to third parties, except to the service providers strictly necessary to deliver the features you requested, who are bound by contract to process it only on our instructions, or where required by law or to investigate abuse.

Admira personnel do not read your Google user data, except with your explicit consent (for example, to resolve a support issue you have raised), where necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or where required by law.

Revoking access and deletion. You can disconnect your Google Ads data source in Admira at any time. When you do, we delete the stored Google credential for that data source and delete the Google Ads data retrieved into your workspace. You can also review and revoke Admira’s access directly in your Google Account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions about this section, or requests relating to Google user data, can be sent to support@getadmira.com.
